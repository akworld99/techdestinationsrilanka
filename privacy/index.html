Privacy

What we collect on this site, why, and how to have it removed.

Last updated 10 October 2026.

Forms

When you send a form on this site we receive what you typed: usually your name, email, company and message. Forms are delivered to us by Formspree, a form handling service. We use the information to reply to you and to make the introductions you asked for. We do not sell it.

Top Talent applications

If you apply to be featured as Sri Lankan Top Talent, we use your application to assess and, with your agreement, write and publish a profile. You see the profile before it is published. We do not publish your email or phone number. You can ask us to remove a published profile at any time.

Newsletter

If you subscribe, we use your email only to send you new stories. Every email will let you unsubscribe.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand which pages are read. For visitors in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, analytics cookies stay off unless you accept them in the banner. You can change your choice by clearing this site's data in your browser.

Embedded LinkedIn posts

Some pages show posts from our LinkedIn page inside a LinkedIn frame. LinkedIn may set its own cookies when those load. Their use is covered by LinkedIn's privacy policy.

Your requests

To see, correct or delete information we hold about you, email techdestinationsrilanka@gmail.com or use the contact page.

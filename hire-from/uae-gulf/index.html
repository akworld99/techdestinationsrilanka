Working hours and time zones

Sri Lanka is 1.5 hours ahead of the UAE and Oman and 2.5 hours ahead of Saudi Arabia, Qatar, Kuwait and Bahrain, all year. With a 9 to 6 day in both places you share 6.5 to 7.5 working hours every day. Note that the working week differs in parts of the Gulf, so check which days overlap.

Common questions

What is the time difference between the UK and Sri Lanka?

Sri Lanka is 4.5 hours ahead of the UK during British Summer Time and 5.5 hours ahead in winter, which gives 3.5 to 4.5 shared working hours a day.

Which UK organisations use Sri Lankan engineering?

The London Stock Exchange Group, JP Morgan Personal Investing through WealthOS, and the UK Ministry of Defence through SimCentric are published examples.

How do UK companies usually hire in Sri Lanka?

Smaller teams go through an employer of record or a partner company. Larger groups set up their own entity, as the London Stock Exchange Group did through MillenniumIT.

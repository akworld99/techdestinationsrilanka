Hire Sri Lankan tech talent from the Netherlands

Dutch companies have chosen Sri Lanka after looking at the alternatives. Gapstars was founded in 2015 by Hugo Hemmen after his research into where to build software teams for European companies pointed to Sri Lanka. It now has 300+ people and supports 75+ companies. ICT Group, a Dutch technology company with 40+ years in automotive software, partnered with Inivos to scale its engineering capacity in Colombo.

Working hours and time zones

Sri Lanka is 3.5 hours ahead of the Netherlands in summer and 4.5 hours ahead in winter. With a 9 to 6 day in both places you share 4.5 to 5.5 working hours every day, from 9am to early afternoon Dutch time.

Common questions

Which Dutch companies build software in Sri Lanka?

Gapstars runs its largest engineering base in Colombo for clients such as Otrium and Dual Inventive, and ICT Group works with Inivos on automotive software.

What is the time difference between the Netherlands and Sri Lanka?

Sri Lanka is 3.5 hours ahead in summer and 4.5 hours ahead in winter, giving 4.5 to 5.5 shared working hours a day.

Do Sri Lankan engineers travel to the Netherlands?

Gapstars regularly flies its Colombo engineers to client headquarters in Europe.

Why Sri Lanka is an ideal tech destination for Nordic companies

Nordic companies have the longest record of building software in Sri Lanka. IFS, founded in Sweden, runs 75% of its global R&D in Colombo. Creative Software has served Swedish, Norwegian and Danish clients for 25 years, including DIPS, Norway's largest health records provider. Tiqri, Norwegian parented, has delivered from Colombo for 15 years. Frontwalker, listed in Stockholm, calls Colombo its global tech center.

Working hours and time zones

Sri Lanka is 3.5 hours ahead of Sweden, Norway and Denmark in summer and 4.5 hours ahead in winter. With a 9 to 6 day in both places you share 4.5 to 5.5 working hours every day. Finland is one hour closer, with 5.5 to 6.5 shared hours.

Common questions

Why do Nordic companies build software in Sri Lanka?

They have found engineers who stay on a product for years and work as part of the home team. IFS has done it for 27 years, Creative Software for 25 and Tiqri for 15.

What is the time difference between Scandinavia and Sri Lanka?

Sri Lanka is 3.5 hours ahead of Sweden, Norway and Denmark in summer and 4.5 hours ahead in winter, giving 4.5 to 5.5 shared working hours a day.

Which Norwegian and Swedish companies have teams in Sri Lanka?

IFS and Frontwalker from Sweden, and Tiqri and DIPS (through Creative Software) from Norway, are published examples.

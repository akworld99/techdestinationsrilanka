Why Sri Lanka / By country

Why Sri Lanka is an ideal tech destination for German companies

German industry already uses software that Sri Lankan engineers help build. Engineers in Colombo contribute to vehicle software for brands including Volkswagen, BMW, Audi and Mercedes through a European automotive supplier. The Synopsys team in Colombo works on verification tools that chip designers use for automotive silicon. Fewer German companies than Nordic or Dutch ones have opened their own teams here so far, which means less competition for the talent.

Work already done from Sri Lanka for Germany

  • Embedded systems, electric vehicle software and software defined vehicle platforms built to strict safety standards.
  • Emulation and prototyping tools at Synopsys, used to verify chips for automotive, 5G and data center applications.
  • Dedicated teams for companies in Austria and the Benelux, a model that transfers directly to German scaleups.

Sourced detail on this work is in our stories.

Working hours and time zones

Sri Lanka is 3.5 hours ahead of Germany in summer and 4.5 hours ahead in winter. With a 9 to 6 day in both places you share 4.5 to 5.5 working hours every day.

Common questions

Do Sri Lankan engineers work on software for German car makers?

Yes, through suppliers. Engineers in Colombo contribute to projects run by ICT Automotive, whose vehicle software is used by brands including Volkswagen, BMW, Audi and Mercedes.

What is the time difference between Germany and Sri Lanka?

Sri Lanka is 3.5 hours ahead in summer and 4.5 hours ahead in winter, giving 4.5 to 5.5 shared working hours a day.

Is German required?

Teams work in English. Companies that need German speaking contact usually keep product ownership in Germany and run engineering in English.

Working hours and time zones

Sri Lanka is 9.5 to 10.5 hours ahead of US Eastern time, so a standard 9 to 6 day in both places does not overlap. Teams that serve US clients work a later shift. A Colombo team working until 9.30pm shares two to three hours with the East Coast morning. Many US companies use the gap on purpose: work handed over at the end of the US day is ready the next morning.

Common questions

How do US companies handle the time difference with Sri Lanka?

Standard working days do not overlap, so Sri Lankan teams serving US clients usually work a later shift that covers the US morning. Others use the gap for overnight progress and hand over at each end of the day.

Which US companies have engineering teams in Sri Lanka?

Sysco, Synopsys, Zebra Technologies and Virtusa all run engineering in Colombo. EY and HSBC also operate there.

Can a US startup hire just one or two people in Sri Lanka?

Yes. You can hire through an employer of record, take contractors, or use a company such as Oceans. Tell us the role and we will point you to the right route.

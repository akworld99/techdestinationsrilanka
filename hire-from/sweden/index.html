Why Sri Lanka is an ideal tech destination for Swedish companies

Sweden's best known enterprise software company does most of its engineering in Sri Lanka. IFS, founded in Sweden, does about 60% of its global product development in Colombo, with 2,200+ people. Frontwalker, listed in Stockholm, calls its Colombo office its global tech center. Creative Software has built dedicated teams for Swedish clients for 25 years.

Common questions

Which Swedish companies have engineering teams in Sri Lanka?

IFS and Frontwalker both run large engineering operations in Colombo. EQT, the Swedish investment firm, owns WSO2 and co owns IFS and IGT1 Lanka.

What is the time difference between Sweden and Sri Lanka?

Sri Lanka is 3.5 hours ahead in summer and 4.5 hours ahead in winter, giving 4.5 to 5.5 shared working hours a day.

How much of IFS is built in Sri Lanka?

About 60% of IFS global product development is done in Colombo by more than 2,200 people, according to IFS.

Sri Lanka for startups: build your engineering team

Startups use Sri Lanka to build a senior engineering team at a fraction of home market cost without lowering the bar. WealthOS built its wealth platform with 50+ engineers in Sri Lanka and was acquired by JPMorganChase less than three years after its first client. Oceans supplies Sri Lankan talent to 600+ US startups, and Gapstars builds engineering teams for Dutch scaleups such as Otrium.

What startups hire first

Usually two to five engineers through an employer of record or a small partner team, alongside a founder or lead at home who owns the product. Operations, finance and support roles often follow.

What it does to your runway

A senior software engineer costs roughly USD 10,800 to 18,600 a year in Sri Lanka including statutory contributions, depending on how far above the local median you pay. The salary guide shows the working.

Making it work

Hire for seniority, overlap a few hours every day, and treat the team as founders treat early employees. Superloop, for one, gives every Colombo employee company stock.

Common questions

Can an early stage startup hire engineers in Sri Lanka?

Yes. Most start with a few people through an employer of record or a small partner team, which needs no local company.

Which startups have built with Sri Lankan engineers?

WealthOS, acquired by JPMorganChase in 2026, built its platform with a Sri Lankan team. Oceans places Sri Lankan talent with US startups such as Morning Brew and beehiiv, and Gapstars works with Dutch scaleups such as Otrium.

How much can a startup save by hiring in Sri Lanka?

Oceans says its clients save 50% to 80% compared with US hires. The Export Development Board cites 40% to 65% for operations compared with the US or UK.

What startups hire first

Usually two to five engineers through an employer of record or a small partner team, alongside a founder or lead at home who owns the product. Operations, finance and support roles often follow.

What it does to your runway

A senior software engineer costs roughly USD 10,800 to 18,600 a year in Sri Lanka including statutory contributions, depending on how far above the local median you pay. The salary guide shows the working.

Making it work

Hire for seniority, overlap a few hours every day, and treat the team as founders treat early employees. Some foreign employers give every Colombo employee company stock.

What this looks like in Sri Lanka today

  • A wealth platform founded by two Sri Lankans in 2019 and acquired by JPMorganChase in 2026.
  • Operators and specialists working inside hundreds of US startups.
  • Embedded engineering teams for European scaleups.
  • A Colombo startup that now builds for European governments.

Common questions

Can an early stage startup hire engineers in Sri Lanka?

Yes. Most start with a few people through an employer of record or a small partner team, which needs no local company.

Which startups have built with Sri Lankan engineers?

A wealth platform acquired by JPMorganChase in 2026 was built with a Sri Lankan team. US startups such as Morning Brew and beehiiv work with Sri Lankan professionals, as do Dutch scaleups.

How much can a startup save by hiring in Sri Lanka?

The Export Development Board cites 40% to 65% for operations compared with the US or UK.

IT staff augmentation in Sri Lanka

Staff augmentation means adding individual Sri Lankan professionals to your existing team, managed by you, without setting up a local company. You can do it through an employer of record, by contracting people directly, or through a talent partner. Oceans has placed Sri Lankan professionals with 600+ US startups and agencies, and reports an 86% first match success rate.

Three ways to do it

An employer of record employs the person in Sri Lanka on your behalf: Deel, Remote, G-P and Multiplier all list the country. A contractor agreement suits short or part time work. A talent partner finds, vets and sometimes trains people for you.

What it costs

On self reported 2026 Glassdoor data a software engineer in Colombo earns a median of about LKR 177,000 a month and a senior engineer about LKR 260,000, with export companies paying well above that. Add 15% for statutory contributions and the provider's fee.

Getting the best people

The strongest engineers have options. Pay on an international benchmark, give them real ownership, and bring them to your headquarters when you can. That is what the companies with ten year retention do.

Common questions

Can I hire a Sri Lankan developer without a local company?

Yes. An employer of record such as Deel, Remote, G-P or Multiplier can employ the person for you, or you can contract them directly.

How much does a remote developer in Sri Lanka cost?

A median of about LKR 177,000 a month for a software engineer and LKR 260,000 for a senior engineer on self reported 2026 Glassdoor data, plus 15% statutory contributions. Export focused employers pay more.

Is staff augmentation or a dedicated team better?

Staff augmentation suits one to ten people you manage directly. A dedicated team suits larger groups where you want a partner to handle hiring, HR and retention.

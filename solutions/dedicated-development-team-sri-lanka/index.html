Dedicated development teams in Sri Lanka

A dedicated development team is a group of engineers employed and managed by a Sri Lankan company who work only on your product, as an extension of your own team. It is the most common way foreign companies start in Sri Lanka. Some of these teams have run for 25 years for Scandinavian clients, and relationships of more than a decade are common.

How it works

You define the roles. The partner recruits, employs and houses the team, handles HR and retention, and bills you monthly per person. You manage the work day to day as you would your own staff. Some clients later take the team into their own entity.

Why companies choose it

You get a working team in weeks without registering a company or learning local employment law. The partner carries the hiring risk. And because the engineers work only for you, knowledge stays with your product.

What to ask a partner

How long do your client teams last? What is your annual attrition? Can I interview every engineer? Who owns the code and the laptops? What happens if I want to take the team in house? Good partners answer these without hesitation.

What this looks like in Sri Lanka today

  • A Colombo team that DIPS of Norway credits as a significant contributor to its health record platform.
  • One long term team per client instead of a rotating pool.
  • Teams that embed in the client's company and regularly travel to its headquarters.
  • Dedicated IT teams for clients in Australia, the US, Europe and the UK.

Common questions

What is a dedicated development team?

A team of engineers employed by a partner company who work only on your product and are managed day to day by you, while the partner handles hiring, HR, office space and retention.

How long does it take to set up a dedicated team in Sri Lanka?

It depends on the roles and the partner. Ask partners for their typical time to hire for your stack, and expect senior and niche roles to take longer.

Which companies offer dedicated development teams in Sri Lanka?

Many Sri Lankan engineering companies do. Tell us the stack and team size and we will introduce the ones that fit.

Can I convert a dedicated team into my own company later?

Often yes, and it is worth agreeing the terms at the start.

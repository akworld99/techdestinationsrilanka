Top 41 startups in Sri Lanka (2026)

Sri Lanka's best known startups include PickMe, the ride hailing app that listed in Colombo in 2024, WealthOS, acquired by J.P. Morgan in 2026, and a wave of fintechs led by PayHere, Mintpay and Koko. Alongside them are AI and deeptech companies such as Logical, backed by Y Combinator, Scouts in drones and Ceylon Graphene Technologies in advanced materials. These 41 tech and tech enabled startups and scale ups were founded in Sri Lanka or by Sri Lankans who build there, and each comes with one sourced fact.

Common questions

What are the best startups in Sri Lanka?

Among the best known are PickMe, the ride hailing and delivery app that listed on the Colombo Stock Exchange in 2024; WealthOS, the wealth management platform acquired by J.P. Morgan in 2026; and fintechs such as PayHere, Mintpay and Koko. WSO2, founded in Colombo, was acquired by EQT in 2024 in a deal reported at about USD 600 million. This page lists 50 with a sourced fact for each.

How many startups are there in Sri Lanka?

Estimates vary with the definition. Our startup ecosystem guide brings together the published counts and rankings, including Startup Genome's 2025 and 2026 reports.

What are the top AI startups in Sri Lanka?

Examples on this page include Logical, the desktop AI assistant founded by Sri Lankan engineers and backed by Y Combinator, Scouts, which builds AI drones for security and agriculture, and SenzMate, which applies AI and IoT to farming and industry.

What are the leading fintech startups in Sri Lanka?

PayHere, Mintpay, Koko, DirectPay, PAYable, WEBXPAY, Onepay and InsureMe are among the most active. WealthOS, a wealth management platform, was acquired by J.P. Morgan in 2026.

Has Sri Lanka produced a unicorn?

Not yet. Its largest technology exits are WSO2, acquired by EQT in 2024 in a deal reported at about USD 600 million, and Virtusa, which was founded with its main delivery base in Colombo and acquired by Baring Private Equity Asia in 2021 for about USD 2 billion.

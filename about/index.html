About

Tech Destination Sri Lanka helps companies abroad hire talent and build teams in Sri Lanka. We lead with sourced reporting, so founders, CTOs and investors can judge the talent on evidence before they talk to us.

What we do

Two things. We report on what Sri Lankan technology talent has built, with sources. And we help companies abroad act on it: finding talent, matching delivery partners, and setting up their own teams. The reporting is how most people find us. The services are how we help once they do.

Beyond technology, we help buyers source products from Sri Lankan exporters, and help Sri Lankan exporters find and win overseas tenders through Tender Radar and bid support.

Why this exists

Sri Lankan engineers built the trading platform the London Stock Exchange bought. They write core R&D for IFS, Sysco and Synopsys. They founded WSO2 and Virtusa. Most of the world has never heard any of it.

We started on LinkedIn in April 2026 to change that, one sourced story at a time. This site is the permanent home for those stories.

How we report

Credibility is the only asset a publication like this has. So we hold every story to the same rules.

  • Every claim needs a source, and the sources are listed at the end of each story.
  • We credit people. The story is what Sri Lankan engineers and founders did, never a slogan about a country.
  • If we cannot verify a number, we ask the company or leave it out.
  • One company, one story. No filler and no buzzwords.
  • When facts change after publication, we update the story and say so.

The full rules, including how we check figures and handle corrections, are in our editorial standards.

Who it is for

Foreign founders, CTOs, hiring managers and investors who are deciding where to build their next team. If that is you, tell us what you need and we will point you to the right people.

Get featured

We feature companies of every size as long as the story holds up, and we feature individual professionals through Sri Lankan Top Talent. Suggest a company or apply as a professional.

What we are not

We are not a government body, an industry association or an investment promotion agency, and we do not speak for any of them.

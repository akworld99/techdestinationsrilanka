Where Romania is stronger

  • EU membership and a European working day.
  • English: 11th in the world on the EF index.
  • Engineering centres for Oracle, Microsoft and Amazon.

Where Sri Lanka is stronger

  • Lower cost at similar workforce size.
  • A 15% tax rate on exported services, against rising labour taxes in Romania.
  • Core R&D for IFS, Sysco and Synopsys, and locally built companies sold to global buyers.

What to watch in each

  • Romania: The IT income tax exemption ended on 1 January 2025 with no replacement, and the industry body says headcount has been flat since 2022. Source
  • Sri Lanka: Outside the EU and three and a half hours ahead of Bucharest in summer.

Is Sri Lanka cheaper than Romania for software development?

Published rates for Sri Lanka are about USD 25 to 40 an hour according to the Export Development Board. For Romania: Senior USD 40 to 55 an hour (Uvik index 2026, vendor data). The sources use different methods, so treat the comparison as indicative and ask for quotes.

How does English in Sri Lanka compare with Romania?

On the EF English Proficiency Index 2025 Sri Lanka ranks 73rd of 123 countries. Romania: 11th of 123, score 605 (very high band). The index tests the general adult population. Sri Lanka's export technology sector works in English.

Sri Lanka compared with other offshore development destinations

Sri Lanka is one of the lower cost offshore destinations, with rates of about USD 25 to 40 an hour, and a small but deep engineering base that runs core R&D for IFS, Sysco and Synopsys. It ranks below Eastern Europe and Southeast Asia on general English and innovation indices, and above them on cost. Common questions

Which country is best for outsourcing software development?

It depends on what you need. Poland and Romania suit buyers who need EU jurisdiction and a European working day. Mexico and Colombia suit US companies that need full day overlap. Vietnam and the Philippines offer scale. Sri Lanka suits long running product engineering, enterprise software and finance work at Asian rates, with an export sector that works in English.

Which offshore destinations are cheapest?

Accelerance's 2026 guide puts senior rates in Asia at USD 31 to 41 an hour, against USD 64 to 76 in Europe and USD 60 to 75 in Latin America. Sri Lanka's Export Development Board cites about USD 25 to 40 an hour.

Is Sri Lanka a recognised outsourcing destination?

Yes. Kearney ranked it 23rd of 60 countries in its 2021 Global Services Location Index, and the UK's National Outsourcing Association named it Outsourcing Destination of the Year in 2013.

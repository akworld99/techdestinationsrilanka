Sri Lanka vs Poland for software development and outsourcing

Poland is Europe's largest nearshore engineering market, inside the EU, with very high English and Google's biggest EU engineering hub. It costs roughly twice as much as Sri Lanka: senior rates of USD 45 to 60 an hour against about USD 25 to 40. Choose Poland when you need EU jurisdiction, the same time zone and short flights. Choose Sri Lanka when budget matters and four to five hours of daily overlap is enough.

Where Poland is stronger

  • EU membership: data protection, contracts and travel are simpler for European buyers.
  • English: 15th in the world on the EF index.
  • Same working day as Western Europe.
  • A workforce of more than half a million ICT specialists.
  • Higher placings on every general ranking.

Where Sri Lanka is stronger

  • Cost: Asian senior rates average USD 31 to 41 an hour against USD 64 to 76 for Europe, according to Accelerance.
  • Retention stories of 15 and 25 years with Nordic clients.
  • Less competition for talent from local big tech.
  • Nordic companies already here at scale, led by IFS.

What to watch in each

  • Poland: Rising wage costs, difficulty accessing IT talent and a stronger currency are squeezing exporters. Source
  • Sri Lanka: Outside the EU, so check data transfer requirements. Three and a half to four and a half hours ahead of Central Europe.

Is Sri Lanka cheaper than Poland for software development?

Published rates for Sri Lanka are about USD 25 to 40 an hour according to the Export Development Board. For Poland: Senior USD 45 to 60 an hour (Uvik index 2026, vendor data). Europe senior average USD 64 to 76 (Accelerance 2026). The sources use different methods, so treat the comparison as indicative and ask for quotes.

How does English in Sri Lanka compare with Poland?

On the EF English Proficiency Index 2025 Sri Lanka ranks 73rd of 123 countries. Poland: 15th of 123, score 600 (very high band). The index tests the general adult population. Sri Lanka's export technology sector works in English.

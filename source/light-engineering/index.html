Pump, engine and light engineering manufacturers in Sri Lanka

Sri Lanka's exports of boilers, piston engines, pumps and vacuum pumps reached US$171.39 million in the first half of 2026 and grew 508.71% in the first nine months. The wider light engineering industry has more than 500 manufacturing units making pumps, agricultural, tea and rubber processing machinery, castings, aluminium extrusions, fasteners, steel structures and auto parts. The Export Development Board says Sri Lankan makers can serve small order and niche requirements. Growth in pumps and engines is from a low base, so check supplier depth case by case.

Why buyers source light engineering and machinery from Sri Lanka

  • Exports of boilers, piston engines, pumps and vacuum pumps reached US$171.39 million in January to June 2026. EDB via Daily FT
  • Exports of the same group to India rose from US$0.24 million in 2024 to US$11.3 million in 2025. EDB India market profile
  • The light engineering industry has more than 500 manufacturing units and employs over 100,000 people directly and indirectly. EDB
  • The Export Development Board says Sri Lanka can cater to small order requirements in niche markets. EDB
  • The US bought US$39.12 million of Sri Lankan nails, screws, bolts and nuts in 2025. EDB US market profile

Certifications and standards to ask for

  • ISO 9001. Held by listed exporters; ask for the certificate for the producing site.
  • Material test certificates. Most makers use metal scrap, so ask for alloy and traceability certificates.
  • CE marking and pump test standards. Machinery for the EU needs CE marking; pump performance tests to ISO 9906 where specified.

Know this before you buy

  • The latest sector wide export figure is US$306 million for 2019; no 2025 total has been published. EDB
  • The jump in pumps and engines comes from a very low base, so supplier depth varies. EDB India market profile

Questions to ask a Sri Lankan engineering manufacturer

  • Which products do you make in house, and which do you assemble?
  • Can you supply material test certificates for each lot?
  • Do your machines carry CE marking, if I sell in the EU?
  • What is your capacity per month for my product?
  • Can you build to my drawings and standards?

Coconut shell activated carbon manufacturers in Sri Lanka

Sri Lanka is among the top three producers of activated carbon in the world, according to the Export Development Board, and exports reached US$155.55 million in 2024. Exports rose 37.79% in the first nine months of 2026. Producers make granular, powdered and pelletised coconut shell activated carbon for water treatment, air and gas treatment, gold recovery, food and beverage and energy storage, and the main markets are the US, the EU, Japan and South Korea. Grade specifications are agreed supplier by supplier, so ask for a certificate of analysis per lot.

Certifications and standards to ask for

  • Certificate of analysis per lot. Iodine number, mesh size, hardness, ash, moisture and CTC as agreed in the contract.
  • Drinking water approvals. For potable water, ask for AWWA B604 conformity or NSF/ANSI 61 certification where your market requires it.
  • Food grade, halal and kosher. Ask for these if the carbon is for food and beverage use.
  • ISO 9001 and ISO 14001. Ask for current certificates for the producing site.

Know this before you buy

  • Shell supply depends on the coconut harvest, which fell to 2,684 million nuts in 2024. Daily FT
  • Sri Lanka also imports shell charcoal and activated carbon. If you need Sri Lankan origin shell, specify it and ask for origin documents. International Coconut Community

Questions to ask a Sri Lankan activated carbon producer

  • Which grades do you make, and what iodine number, mesh and hardness can you guarantee?
  • Do you activate from shell charcoal you make yourself?
  • Can you supply AWWA B604 or NSF/ANSI 61 documentation for drinking water use?
  • Do you make impregnated or washed grades?
  • What is your monthly capacity per grade?

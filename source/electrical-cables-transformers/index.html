Source from Sri Lanka

Electrical cable, transformer and switchgear manufacturers in Sri Lanka

Sri Lanka's electrical and electronic component exports reached US$508.86 million in the first nine months of 2026, up 61% and already above the US$438.49 million earned in all of 2025. The Export Development Board credits insulated wires and cables (+82.59%), transformers (+33.46%), switches, boards and panels (+31.49%), telephone and audio equipment (+234.82%) and boilers, engines and pumps (+508.71%). More than 100 manufacturers and service providers work across electronics manufacturing services, components, own products and design, and a Sri Lankan transformer maker has won national utility tenders abroad for 1,040 units in 2014 and 1,000 units in 2017.

Why buyers source electrical and electronics from Sri Lanka

  • Electrical and electronic component exports reached US$508.86 million in January to September 2026, up 61.05%, with insulated wires and cables up 82.59%, transformers 33.46%, switches, boards and panels 31.49%, and boilers, engines and pumps 508.71%. EDB via Newswire
  • The sector has more than 100 manufacturers and service providers across electronics manufacturing services, component manufacturing, own products and design. EDB
  • The sector earned US$438.49 million in 2025 and employs 37,000 people. EDB
  • Transformer, coil and inductor makers state conformity with ISO 9001, ISO 14001 and the EU RoHS directive. EDB
  • A Sri Lankan transformer maker won a national utility tender for 1,040 distribution transformers in South Asia in 2014. Daily FT
  • The same maker won a contract for 1,000 distribution transformers from an electricity distribution company in South Asia in 2017. Daily FT
  • A Sri Lankan cable maker exports to Sweden, the UK, Africa, the Maldives, Australia, New Zealand and the Caribbean. EDB

Certifications and standards to ask for

  • IEC 60076. The standard power and distribution transformer tenders specify, with type test reports often no older than five years.
  • IEC 60502. Power cables, with conductors to IEC 60228.
  • IEC 61439. Low voltage switchgear and controlgear assemblies, with type test or design verification certificates.
  • Accredited type tests. Short circuit and other type tests at an ISO/IEC 17025 accredited laboratory; ask which ratings each report covers.
  • ISO 9001, ISO 14001 and RoHS. Held by transformer and coil makers, according to the Export Development Board.

Know this before you buy

  • Monthly figures swing: exports fell 11.09% in December 2025, then rose 88.35% in September 2026. EDB via Daily FT
  • Local product testing services are limited, so ask for third party type test reports rather than relying on local tests. Central Bank of Sri Lanka
  • Several of the largest exporters are units of foreign groups, so check that a claim describes the Sri Lankan plant, not the parent group. EDB

Questions to ask a Sri Lankan electrical manufacturer

  • Which ratings and voltage classes do your type test reports cover, and from which lab?
  • Which IEC or BS standards do you build to for this product?
  • Can you share supply records for similar units in the last three years?
  • Will you allow our engineers to witness factory acceptance tests?
  • What are your guaranteed losses and the tolerance on them?

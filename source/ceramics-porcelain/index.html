Porcelain tableware and ceramics manufacturers in Sri Lanka

Sri Lanka exported US$20.76 million of tableware and kitchenware in 2024, mostly porcelain and fine china, along with wall and floor tiles and ornamental ware. The Export Development Board says Sri Lankan tableware conforms to US FDA norms and ASTM standards, with in glaze, on glaze and under glaze decoration, heavy metal free designs and microwave safe gold and platinum. The industry uses local quartz, feldspar, dolomite and china clay and employs more than 20,000 people. Main tableware markets include India, the US and Japan.

Why buyers source ceramics and porcelain from Sri Lanka

  • Porcelain and china tableware exports were US$18.97 million in 2024, within US$20.76 million of tableware and kitchenware. EDB ceramic report 2025
  • Sri Lankan tableware conforms to US FDA norms and ASTM standards. EDB
  • Decoration includes in glaze, on glaze and under glaze, heavy metal free decoration and microwave safe gold and platinum. EDB
  • The industry uses local quartz, feldspar, dolomite and china clay. EDB
  • One listed porcelain exporter has capacity of 8.4 million pieces a year and ships to more than 44 countries. EDB

Certifications and standards to ask for

  • Lead and cadmium leach tests. To US FDA limits, or EU rules for Europe, for your designs.
  • ISO 13006. For ceramic tiles.
  • ISO 9001 and ISO 14001. Held by listed tableware exporters.

Know this before you buy

  • Tableware and kitchenware exports fell from US$27.45 million in 2022 to US$20.76 million in 2024. EDB ceramic report 2025
  • The industry is concentrated in a few key tableware makers, so capacity for large hotel programmes needs early booking. EDB ceramic report 2025

Questions to ask a Sri Lankan porcelain manufacturer

  • Can you share leach test reports for these decorations?
  • What is your capacity and lead time for a hotel programme?
  • Can you develop my own shapes and decals?
  • Do you hold stock patterns for repeat orders?
  • How do you pack against breakage for sea freight?

Common questions

What ceramic products does Sri Lanka export?

Porcelain and fine china tableware and kitchenware, other ceramic tableware, wall and floor tiles, roofing tiles, ornaments and bathware.

Is Sri Lankan porcelain tableware food safe?

The Export Development Board says it conforms to US FDA norms and ASTM standards, with heavy metal free decorations available. Ask each supplier for leach test reports on your designs.

Where does Sri Lankan tableware sell?

India, the US, Japan, the UAE, the UK, Germany, Kenya, Italy, Australia and Mexico are the top tableware markets.

Printing and packaging companies in Sri Lanka: cartons, labels and book printing

Sri Lankan printers and packaging makers supply tea cartons, printed paperboard boxes, corrugated cartons, labels and garment tags, paper sacks and recycled kraft paper, and print books, catalogues, stationery and calendars on flexo and offset presses. The industry has more than 50 years of prepress experience, and export revenue was an estimated US$118 million in 2019, the latest sector total on the Export Development Board's pages. Much of the packaging serves Sri Lanka's own tea and apparel exporters. Certificates such as FSC, BRCGS or food contact compliance vary by company.

Why buyers source printing and packaging from Sri Lanka

  • Sri Lanka's printing industry has more than 50 years of prepress experience. EDB
  • Printing and paper product exports grew from US$83 million in 2015 to an estimated US$118 million in 2019. EDB
  • Export packaging lines include tea cartons, apparel inner cartons, printed paperboard boxes and corrugated cartons, on flexo and offset. EDB
  • A listed kraft paper producer states all its products are FSC certified for export packaging. EDB

Certifications and standards to ask for

  • FSC chain of custody. Check the certificate code on the FSC database.
  • BRCGS Packaging Materials or ISO 22000. For food and tea packaging.
  • EU food contact compliance. Regulation 1935/2004 and good manufacturing practice for packaging that touches food.
  • Colour management. Ask for ISO 12647 or G7 if colour matching matters to you.

Know this before you buy

  • Sector export figures are dated, with 2019 the latest total, and differ between official pages. EDB
  • No ISO, BRCGS or food contact certificates are listed sector wide, so verify each supplier. EDB

Questions to ask a Sri Lankan printing and packaging company

  • Which certificates do you hold: FSC, BRCGS Packaging, ISO 22000?
  • Which presses and finishing do you run, and what sizes?
  • Can you proof to my colour standard?
  • What are your minimum runs and lead times?
  • Can you consolidate packaging with my product shipment from Sri Lanka?

Common questions

What printing and packaging does Sri Lanka export?

Books, catalogues and stationery; tea cartons, printed boxes, corrugated cartons, labels and garment tags; paper sacks; and recycled kraft paper.

Can Sri Lankan suppliers provide FSC certified packaging?

Some can. At least one kraft paper producer states all its products are FSC certified. Ask each supplier for its chain of custody code.

Which markets do Sri Lankan printers serve?

Mainly the EU, plus the UK, the US, Australia, Singapore, India, the Maldives and Bangladesh.

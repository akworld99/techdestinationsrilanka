How to import from Sri Lanka: trade deals, documents, shipping and payment

Importing from Sri Lanka means agreeing the specification and terms with an exporter, shipping from the Port of Colombo or by air, and using a certificate of origin to claim lower duties where your country offers them. The EU removes duties on 66% of tariff lines under GSP+, the UK gives zero duty on more than 7,000 product lines, and India admits most Sri Lankan goods duty free. Most US imports from Sri Lanka pay a 10% Section 301 tariff on top of normal duties since July 2026. Colombo handled a record 8.29 million TEUs in 2025.

Your first order from Sri Lanka, step by step

  1. Define what you are buying

    Product, grade, packing, labelling, quantity and the certificates you need. Ask your customs broker for the HS code and your duty rate, with and without a trade preference.

  2. Shortlist and check exporters

    Check registration, certificates and export history. The Export Development Board's directory and our request for quote are two places to start.

  3. Samples and price

    Approve samples in writing and agree the price on clear delivery terms, such as FOB Colombo or CIF to your port.

  4. Contract and payment

    Agree inspection, payment terms such as a letter of credit or documents against payment, and what happens if a shipment fails inspection.

  5. Documents

    Commercial invoice, packing list, bill of lading or air waybill, certificate of origin, and the product certificates your market needs.

  6. Ship and clear

    Ship by sea from Colombo or by air from Bandaranaike International Airport, and clear customs with the certificate of origin to claim any preference.

Duties and trade agreements

Your duty depends on the product, your country and whether the goods qualify as Sri Lankan origin.

  • Sri Lanka has had EU GSP+ since 19 May 2017. It fully removes EU duties on 66% of tariff lines, including textiles and fisheries. European Commission
  • In 2025, 69% of eligible Sri Lankan exports to the EU used GSP+; apparel was 48% of preferential imports, rubber 17% and food preparations 12%. GSP Hub
  • New EU GSP rules apply from 1 January 2027. Countries that are GSP+ beneficiaries on 31 December 2026 must reapply by 31 December 2028. Daily Mirror
  • Sri Lanka is in the Enhanced Preferences tier of the UK Developing Countries Trading Scheme, with zero duty on more than 7,000 product lines. EDB
  • Relaxed UK rules of origin, due to take effect in early 2026, let Sri Lankan garment makers use inputs from any country and keep duty free access. Ada Derana
  • Under the India Sri Lanka Free Trade Agreement, in force since 1 March 2000, Sri Lankan origin goods enter India duty free except a negative list of 429 tariff lines and quota items such as garments, tea, pepper and desiccated coconut. Goods need at least 35% domestic value addition. EDB
  • The Pakistan Sri Lanka Free Trade Agreement has operated since June 2005, with a 10,000 tonne a year duty free quota for tea. EDB
  • The Singapore Sri Lanka Free Trade Agreement, in force since May 2018, removes tariffs on 80% of tariff lines over up to 15 years. Enterprise Singapore
  • A free trade agreement with Thailand was signed in February 2024; check its status for your product before you rely on it. Hiru News
  • The US set a 20% reciprocal tariff on Sri Lanka in August 2025. After the Supreme Court struck down those tariffs in February 2026, a temporary 10% tariff applied. Daily FT
  • In July 2026 the US placed Sri Lanka in its 10% Section 301 tariff category, added on top of normal customs duties. News First

Export documents you will receive

  • The Department of Commerce issues certificates of origin under all GSP schemes and free trade agreements, usually within two hours for air and three for sea freight. Department of Commerce
  • Non preferential certificates of origin come from the chambers of commerce. EDB
  • The National Plant Quarantine Service issues phytosanitary certificates on the IPPC model, after the exporter registers and books an inspection. Department of Agriculture
  • Health certificates for fish and animal products come from the animal health and fisheries authorities, and for food from the Ministry of Health's Food Control Administrative Division. EDB export procedure
  • Cinnamon and cashew need a compulsory pre shipment quality certificate. EDB export procedure
  • The export declaration is the CusDec, filed online by the exporter or a customs house agent, and goods must ship within 30 days of registration. Sri Lanka Customs

Shipping from Sri Lanka

  • The Port of Colombo handled a record 8,291,178 TEUs in 2025. Sri Lanka Ports Authority
  • About 80% of Colombo's container volume in January to October 2025 was transhipment. Maritime Gateway
  • Colombo rose to 20th among the world's container ports in the first half of 2026. Daily FT
  • In September 2026 yard operations ran at 125% of capacity, so allow for congestion in your lead times. Daily Mirror

Payment terms

  • Sri Lankan exporters use advance payment, letters of credit, documents against payment, documents against acceptance and open account. EDB
  • Under documents against payment, the buyer's bank releases the shipping documents only after payment. EDB
  • Exporters must bring export proceeds into Sri Lanka within 180 days of shipment. Daily FT
  • Since June 2026 exporters must convert remaining export proceeds into rupees by the 10th of the following month. News First

How to check a Sri Lankan exporter

  • The Export Development Board's exporters directory lists each exporter's details and products. Listing is voluntary, and exporters submit certifications and clearances to be listed. EDB
  • Exporters register with Sri Lanka Customs and the Inland Revenue Department; Export Development Board registration is voluntary. EDB
  • Foreign buyers can register with the Export Development Board for sourcing help and matchmaking. EDB

Common questions

Is it duty free to import from Sri Lanka to the UK?

Many products are. Sri Lanka is in the Enhanced Preferences tier of the UK Developing Countries Trading Scheme, with zero duty on more than 7,000 product lines, as long as the goods meet the rules of origin.

What is the import duty from Sri Lanka to India?

Under the India Sri Lanka Free Trade Agreement, Sri Lankan origin goods enter India duty free except a negative list of 429 tariff lines and quota items such as garments, tea, pepper and desiccated coconut. Goods need at least 35% Sri Lankan value addition.

What tariff does the US charge on Sri Lankan goods?

In July 2026 Sri Lanka was placed in the 10% Section 301 tariff category, added on top of normal customs duties. Rates have changed several times since 2025, so check the current rate with your customs broker.

Does Sri Lanka still have GSP+ with the EU?

Yes. Sri Lanka has had GSP+ since 2017. New EU GSP rules apply from 1 January 2027, and Sri Lanka must reapply by the end of 2028.

Who issues certificates of origin in Sri Lanka?

The Department of Commerce issues preferential certificates of origin under GSP schemes and free trade agreements; chambers of commerce issue non preferential ones.

How do I pay a Sri Lankan exporter safely?

On a first order, a letter of credit that requires an independent inspection certificate gives the buyer most control. Documents against payment protects the seller more than the buyer. Move to open account once trust is built.

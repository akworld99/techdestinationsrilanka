Rubber glove manufacturers in Sri Lanka: examination, surgical, industrial and household gloves

Sri Lanka exported US$293 million of industrial and surgical rubber gloves in 2025, its second largest rubber product line after tyres, and glove exports rose 7.58% in the first nine months of 2026. Manufacturers make gloves for healthcare, industrial, household and agricultural use, as well as seals, gaskets, washers, mats, rubber bands and hoses, using Sri Lankan natural rubber including latex crepe. About 75 rubber product exporters are registered with the Board of Investment. For medical gloves in the UK or EU, ask for EN 455 test reports.

Certifications and standards to ask for

  • EN 455 and AQL. For medical gloves in the UK and EU: EN 455 parts 1 to 4, typically with an AQL of 1.5 for freedom from holes. Ask for the test reports.
  • CE or UKCA marking. Required for medical and protective gloves sold in the EU and UK, with the notified body number where it applies.
  • ISO 9001 and ISO 14001. Ask for current certificates for the plant that will make your order.

Know this before you buy

  • The Export Development Board lists the high cost of testing and certification as a sector weakness, so confirm which certificates a factory already holds. EDB rubber report 2026
  • Natural rubber supply and prices swing; the Export Development Board lists inadequate raw material and price fluctuations as weaknesses. EDB rubber report 2026
  • In July 2026 the US placed Sri Lanka in its 10% Section 301 tariff category, added on top of normal customs duties. Rates have changed several times since 2025, so check the current rate before you cost a landed price. News First

Questions to ask a Sri Lankan glove manufacturer

  • Which glove types, materials, sizes and thicknesses do you make?
  • Can you share EN 455 test reports from an accredited lab, and your AQL levels?
  • Is the glove CE or UKCA marked, and which notified body is involved?
  • What are your monthly capacity and lead times?
  • Do you pack under private label?

Common questions

Which HS codes apply to rubber gloves?

Medical, surgical and examination gloves of vulcanised rubber are HS 4015.12. Household and industrial rubber gloves are 4015.19. Gaskets and seals are 4016.93 and rubber mats 4016.91.

What kinds of gloves does Sri Lanka export?

Gloves for industrial, household, agricultural and healthcare use. Industrial and surgical gloves earned US$293 million in 2025.

Which standards should I ask a glove supplier for?

For medical gloves sold in the UK or EU, EN 455 test reports and CE or UKCA marking. For chemical protection, EN ISO 374. Ask which certificates the factory already holds before you order.

Where do Sri Lankan rubber gloves go?

UN trade data for 2025 show rubber apparel articles, which include gloves, worth US$74.28 million to the US, US$25.56 million to Germany, US$17.05 million to the UK and US$16.20 million to India.

Ceylon cinnamon suppliers and exporters in Sri Lanka: Alba quills, powder and oils

Sri Lanka is the world's largest producer and exporter of true cinnamon, Cinnamomum zeylanicum, and Ceylon cinnamon has been a protected geographical indication in the EU since February 2022. Cinnamon exports reached US$221.56 million in the first nine months of 2026, up 31%. Quills are graded by diameter into Alba, Continental, Mexican and Hamburg, and every export of quills, chips and powder must meet the national standard SLS 81:2010 with a clearance certificate. The largest markets are Mexico, the US and Peru.

Why buyers source Ceylon cinnamon from Sri Lanka

  • The EU registered Ceylon cinnamon as a protected geographical indication under Regulation (EU) 2022/144, Sri Lanka's first. EUR-Lex
  • The PGI mark covers cut quills, powder, leaf oil and bark oil made only from 100% Ceylon cinnamon grown and processed in Sri Lanka. EDB
  • The Export Development Board says Sri Lanka is the largest producer and exporter of cinnamon, with 90% of the global market. EDB
  • Quills are graded by diameter into Alba, Continental, Mexican and Hamburg; Alba, the most expensive, has a 6 mm quill. EDB
  • Since 2014, quills, chips and crushed or ground cinnamon can only be exported if they meet SLS 81:2010 and carry a Sri Lanka Standards Institution clearance certificate. Gazette 1813/15
  • Cinnamon exports to Mexico were US$91.0 million in 2025, the top product Sri Lanka ships there. EDB Mexico profile

Certifications and standards to ask for

  • SLS 81:2010 clearance. Mandatory for every export of quills, chips and powder.
  • EU PGI mark. Licensed to members of the Ceylon cinnamon PGI association for value added packs.
  • Pure Ceylon Cinnamon trademark. Registered in the EU, Mexico, the US, Peru and Colombia to set it apart from cassia.
  • Certificate of origin, quarantine and quality certificates. Standard documents for spice exports.
  • GMP, HACCP, ISO 22000 and organic. Ask which the processor holds; organic cinnamon has its own tariff lines.

Know this before you buy

  • Farm gate Alba prices hit a record Rs 7,500 a kg in July 2026 as demand climbed, so lock prices and volumes early. Daily Mirror
  • Share figures depend on whether cassia is counted and on the year: the Export Development Board says 90% of the global market, but an older EDB page gives 41% of world cinnamon exports. EDB
  • Ceylon cinnamon is sold as low in coumarin, but no figure is published, so ask for lab results if you need a coumarin limit. EDB

Questions to ask a Sri Lankan cinnamon exporter

  • Which grades can you supply each month, and at what moisture?
  • Can you share the SLS clearance certificate and a recent lab report, including coumarin?
  • Are you a member of the PGI association, if I want the PGI mark on retail packs?
  • Do you process powder in house, and is it sterilised?
  • Which organic and food safety certificates do you hold?

Common questions

What is Alba grade cinnamon?

The thinnest and most expensive grade of Ceylon cinnamon quill, with a diameter of about 6 mm. The other quill grades, by increasing diameter, are Continental, Mexican and Hamburg.

How do I import Ceylon cinnamon in bulk?

Agree grade, moisture and packing with an exporter, who obtains the Sri Lanka Standards Institution clearance under SLS 81:2010 and a certificate of origin. Then check your own market's food rules. Tell us your grade and volume and we will introduce exporters.

How can I tell Ceylon cinnamon from cassia?

Ceylon cinnamon is Cinnamomum zeylanicum. Packs with the EU PGI mark must contain 100% Ceylon cinnamon grown and processed in Sri Lanka, and the Pure Ceylon Cinnamon trademark is registered in the EU, Mexico, the US, Peru and Colombia.

Which HS codes cover Ceylon cinnamon?

Quills, quillings, featherings and chips are 0906.11, crushed cinnamon 0906.20.10, powder 0906.20.20, leaf oil 3301.90.30 and bark oil 3301.90.40.

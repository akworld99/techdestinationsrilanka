Ayurvedic and herbal product manufacturers in Sri Lanka: herbal teas, oils and natural cosmetics

Sri Lankan manufacturers make herbal teas such as Ranawara, Gotukola, Moringa and Bael flower, ayurvedic massage oils and balms, herbal soaps and hair care, and natural cosmetics, many using Sri Lankan spice oils. Ayurveda is governed by the Ayurveda Act of 1961 and the Department of Ayurveda, and the National Medicines Regulatory Authority covers some products. It is a small, developing export category and we found no separate official export figures, so check each manufacturer's certificates and how its products are classified in your market.

Why buyers source Ayurveda and herbal products from Sri Lanka

  • Ayurveda in Sri Lanka is governed by the Ayurveda Act, No. 31 of 1961, administered by the Department of Ayurveda. EDB
  • In July 2025 the Export Development Board ran an export workshop on ayurvedic and herbal cosmetics with the medicines regulator and the Industrial Technology Institute. EDB
  • Ceylon herbal teas include Ranawara, Gotukola, Moringa, Coriander, Bael flower and Hibiscus. EDB
  • Sri Lankan essential oils go mainly into perfumery, cosmetics and toiletries. EDB

Certifications and standards to ask for

  • GMP and HACCP. Ask for current certificates for the plant that makes your product.
  • Organic. Only where the product claims it; ask for the certificate and scope.
  • Your market's rules. Check whether each product is a cosmetic, a food or a herbal medicine where you sell it, before you order.

Know this before you buy

  • No separate official export figures were found for ayurvedic and herbal products. EDB
  • Some products carry health claims that many markets regulate as medicines. Check classification and claims against your own rules. EDB eMarketplace

Questions to ask a Sri Lankan herbal products manufacturer

  • Which products do you make in house, and which do you buy in?
  • Do you hold GMP, and does it cover the products I want?
  • Can you supply full ingredient lists and test reports for my market?
  • Do you make private label, and what are the minimum orders?
  • Which claims are on the pack, and are they allowed where I sell?

Common questions

What ayurvedic and herbal products can I source from Sri Lanka?

Herbal teas, massage oils and balms, herbal soaps, toothpaste, hair and body care, ayurvedic capsules and elixirs, and tea based cosmetics.

Who regulates ayurvedic products in Sri Lanka?

The Department of Ayurveda under the Ayurveda Act of 1961, with the National Medicines Regulatory Authority and the Consumer Affairs Authority involved for some products.

Can I get private label herbal tea or cosmetics?

Some manufacturers make private label. Tell us the product, market and volumes and we will check certificates before introducing anyone.

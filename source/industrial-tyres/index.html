Solid tyre manufacturers in Sri Lanka: forklift, industrial and OTR tyres

Sri Lanka is the world's leading solid tyre exporter, with about 25% of world demand by the Export Development Board's estimate. Tyres and tubes earned US$522 million in 2025, the largest rubber product line, and solid tyres make up 61% of tyre export value. Manufacturers, including joint ventures with international tyre makers, also produce pneumatic and semi pneumatic tyres, tubes and retreading material. Solid tyres go mainly to the US and the EU; most pneumatic tyre exports go to Asia Pacific.

Why buyers source industrial and solid tyres from Sri Lanka

  • The Export Development Board describes Sri Lanka as the leading solid tyre exporter in the world, at about 25% of world demand. EDB rubber report 2026
  • Tyres and tubes earned US$522 million of the US$915 million rubber finished products total in 2025. EDB rubber report 2026
  • Solid tyres account for 61% of tyre export value and new pneumatic tyres for 38%. EDB
  • The industry includes joint ventures between Sri Lankan companies and international tyre makers, and also produces pneumatic and semi pneumatic tyres, tubes and retreading material. EDB
  • Pneumatic and retreaded tyre and tube exports reached US$54.95 million in September 2026, up 39.57% on a year earlier. EDB via Newswire

Certifications and standards to ask for

  • EU REACH. The Export Development Board says Sri Lankan tyres are made under EU REACH rules. Ask each supplier for its substances of very high concern declaration.
  • ISO 9001 and ISO 14001. Ask for current certificates for the plant that will make your tyres.
  • Test reports for your market. Load and speed ratings, and E-mark or DOT marking where your market requires it for pneumatic tyres.

Know this before you buy

  • The Export Development Board's own estimate of Sri Lanka's solid tyre share ranges from 14% to about 25% across its documents. Treat it as an estimate. EDB rubber report 2017
  • Tyre and tube exports fell to US$522 million in 2025 from US$599 million in 2024, before rising again in 2026. EDB rubber report 2026
  • In July 2026 the US placed Sri Lanka in its 10% Section 301 tariff category, added on top of normal customs duties. Rates have changed several times since 2025, so check the current rate before you cost a landed price. News First

Questions to ask a Sri Lankan tyre manufacturer

  • Which tyre sizes, compounds and load ratings do you make, including non marking compounds?
  • Do you make press on bands and resilient tyres for my machine models?
  • Can you provide REACH declarations and test reports for my market?
  • What is the minimum order per size, and the lead time for a mixed container?
  • Do you offer private label or my own branding on the sidewall?

Common questions

What HS code covers solid forklift tyres?

Solid and cushion tyres, including press on bands, come under HS 4012.90. New pneumatic tyres for industrial handling machines are 4011.80 and for agricultural machines 4011.70. Confirm the national tariff line with your customs broker.

Which countries buy Sri Lankan solid tyres?

The Export Development Board names the US and EU countries as the main buyers. UN trade data for 2025 show exports in the tyre group that includes solid tyres of US$140.24 million to the US and US$39.52 million to Germany.

Does Sri Lanka also make pneumatic tyres?

Yes. Manufacturers also make pneumatic and semi pneumatic tyres, tubes and retreading material, and most pneumatic tyre exports go to Asia Pacific.

Do Sri Lankan tyres meet EU chemical rules?

The Export Development Board says they are made under EU REACH regulation. Ask each supplier for its own REACH documentation and test reports.

Footwear and leather goods manufacturers in Sri Lanka

Sri Lanka's footwear industry can make 25 to 35 million pairs a year, plus 30 to 35 million pairs of rubber thongs, according to the Export Development Board. It makes leather and canvas shoes, safety shoes, rubber boots and sports shoes, and small leather goods such as wallets, belts and bags. Sri Lanka is the only producer of sole crepe, a natural rubber used for unit soles. The sector is small, at US$46.4 million of exports in 2023, and most leather is imported, so check materials and capacity early.

Why buyers source footwear and leather goods from Sri Lanka

  • Sri Lanka is the only producer of sole crepe, a natural rubber used for unit soles. EDB footwear brochure
  • Footwear capacity is estimated at 25 to 35 million pairs a year, plus 30 to 35 million pairs of rubber thongs. EDB footwear report 2024
  • Sailing time from Colombo to London is about 15 days. EDB footwear report 2024
  • Value addition in the footwear sector is 35% to 45%. EDB footwear report 2024
  • Sri Lanka has five chrome leather tanneries and one vegetable leather tannery. EDB footwear report 2024

Certifications and standards to ask for

  • Social compliance audit. The Export Development Board cites adherence to international labour practices; ask for the factory's latest audit.
  • Safety footwear standard. For safety shoes, ask for test reports to the standard your market uses, such as EN ISO 20345.
  • Material declarations. Leather origin and chemical compliance, such as REACH for the EU.

Know this before you buy

  • Most leather goods are made from imported leather because local supply of tanned leather is inadequate. EDB footwear report 2024
  • Exports fell from US$140 million in 2016 to US$46.4 million in 2023, so the supplier base is small. EDB footwear report 2024
  • Most sole makers serve only their own shoe production, so independent component capacity is limited. EDB footwear report 2024

Questions to ask a Sri Lankan footwear manufacturer

  • Which footwear types and constructions do you make?
  • Where does your leather come from, and can you document it?
  • What is the minimum order per style and size run?
  • Do you have test reports for safety footwear in my market?
  • Can you use sole crepe or natural rubber soles for my range?

Common questions

What footwear and leather products does Sri Lanka export?

Canvas and rubber boots, thongs, sports shoes, leather shoes and safety shoes, plus small leather goods such as gloves, wallets, bags, purses and belts.

Is the leather made in Sri Lanka?

Mostly not. The Export Development Board says the bulk of leather products are made from imported leather, which comes in duty free.

What is sole crepe?

A natural rubber used to make unit soles. Sri Lanka is its only producer.

How big is the footwear sector?

Small: US$25.93 million of footwear and US$20.47 million of leather products in 2023, with capacity for 25 to 35 million pairs a year.

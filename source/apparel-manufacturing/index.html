Apparel manufacturers in Sri Lanka: activewear, intimates, swimwear and workwear

Apparel is Sri Lanka's largest export, at US$4.91 billion for apparel and textiles in 2025. Manufacturers make sportswear and activewear, lingerie and intimate wear, swimwear, loungewear, bridal wear, workwear and children's wear, mostly for the US, the EU and the UK. The industry sells on ethical and green manufacturing: 17 factories are LEED certified, and Garments Without Guilt certifies labour standards. Exports are down 5.68% in the first nine months of 2026 as US and EU demand softened, so some factories may have spare capacity.

Why buyers source apparel and textiles from Sri Lanka

  • 17 Sri Lankan apparel manufacturing facilities are LEED certified. Sri Lanka Apparel Exporters Association
  • Sri Lanka has the world's first manufacturing facility certified Net Zero Carbon. Board of Investment
  • The range covers sportswear, lingerie, loungewear, bridal wear, workwear, swimwear and children's wear. EDB apparel report 2025
  • The industry commits to ethical manufacturing, free of discrimination, sweatshop practices and child labour, under the Garments Without Guilt banner. EDB apparel report 2025
  • In 2025 Sri Lanka exported US$1.95 billion of apparel to the US, US$1.58 billion to the EU and US$680 million to the UK. Sri Lanka Apparel Exporters Association

Certifications and standards to ask for

  • Garments Without Guilt. An ethical compliance certification issued by the Joint Apparel Association Forum.
  • WRAP. Compliance with lawful, humane and ethical manufacturing.
  • GOTS, Fair Trade and Higg Index. Listed among certifications held across the industry; ask which apply to the factory you use.
  • LEED and carbon neutral certification. For brands that report on factory emissions.
  • ISO 9001 and ISO 14001. Quality and environmental management.

Know this before you buy

  • Apparel and textile exports fell 5.68% in January to September 2026, linked to weaker demand in the US and EU. EDB via Newswire
  • Most export clothing factories are small and medium firms spread across the country, so capacity and compliance vary widely between suppliers. EDB apparel report 2025
  • In July 2026 the US placed Sri Lanka in its 10% Section 301 tariff category, added on top of normal customs duties. Rates have changed several times since 2025, so check the current rate before you cost a landed price. News First

Questions to ask a Sri Lankan apparel manufacturer

  • Which product categories and fabrics do you specialise in, and for which markets?
  • What are your minimum order quantities per style and colour?
  • Which certifications does the factory hold, and can I see the latest social audit?
  • Do you handle development and sampling in house?
  • Where do you source fabric and trims, and can you work with my nominated mills?

Common questions

How do I find an apparel manufacturer in Sri Lanka?

Start from the product: activewear, intimates, swimwear, workwear or children's wear. The Sri Lanka Apparel Exporters Association's member directory can be filtered by product, or tell us your category, volumes and target price and we will introduce manufacturers who fit.

What does Sri Lanka's apparel industry specialise in?

Sportswear and activewear, lingerie and intimate wear, loungewear, bridal wear, workwear, swimwear and children's wear, according to the Export Development Board.

What is Garments Without Guilt?

An ethical compliance certification issued by the Joint Apparel Association Forum, covering labour standards in Sri Lankan apparel factories.

Which markets buy the most Sri Lankan apparel?

In 2025 the US took US$1.95 billion, the EU US$1.58 billion and the UK US$680 million.

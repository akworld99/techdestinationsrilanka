Import from Sri Lanka to India: what sells, ISFTA duty and import rules

India is Sri Lanka's second largest market. It bought US$1.04 billion of Sri Lankan goods in 2025, up 17.21%, led by petroleum oils, animal feed, pepper, areca nuts and animal and vegetable fats and oils. Under the India and Sri Lanka Free Trade Agreement, in force since March 2000, most Sri Lankan goods enter duty free if they meet its rules of origin, except 429 lines on India's negative list; pepper, garments, tea and desiccated coconut get preferences only within annual quotas. Food imports clear through FSSAI's online import clearance system.

Duty and trade access

  • The India and Sri Lanka Free Trade Agreement has been in force since 1 March 2000. India's negative list has 429 tariff lines. To qualify, goods need a change of tariff heading and 35% value addition, or 25% in Sri Lanka where Indian inputs bring the total to 35%. EDB, ISFTA profile
  • India's annual ISFTA quotas: vanaspati, bakery shortening and margarine 250,000 tonnes at zero duty, pepper 2,500 tonnes at zero duty, garments 8 million pieces at zero duty, desiccated coconut 500 tonnes at a 30% margin of preference and tea 15,000 tonnes at 7.5% duty. EDB, ISFTA profile
  • Exports to India rose 12.3% in January to September 2026, to US$889.84 million. EDB, January to September 2026

Import rules to check in India

  • Food imports are cleared through FSSAI's Food Import Clearance System, linked to Customs ICEGate: importers apply online, book the joint inspection and have samples sent to labs through it. FSSAI
  • India set a minimum import price of Rs 500 per kg CIF for black pepper; imports below it are prohibited, as the government told Parliament in February 2021. Confirm the current rule with your customs broker. Lok Sabha answer, 3 February 2021

Common questions

What is the import duty from Sri Lanka to India?

Under the India and Sri Lanka Free Trade Agreement, most Sri Lankan goods enter duty free if they meet its rules of origin: a change of tariff heading and 35% value addition, or 25% where Indian inputs bring the total to 35%. The exceptions are 429 tariff lines on India's negative list, and pepper, garments, tea and desiccated coconut get preferences only within annual quotas.

What does India import from Sri Lanka?

In 2025 the largest lines were petroleum oils, animal feed, pepper, areca nuts, fats and oils, base metal products, women's outerwear, non-alcoholic beverages, coconut milk powder and paper.

Is there a minimum import price for black pepper from Sri Lanka?

India set a minimum import price of Rs 500 per kg CIF for black pepper, and imports below it are prohibited, as the government told Parliament in 2021. Confirm the current rule with your customs broker before you contract.

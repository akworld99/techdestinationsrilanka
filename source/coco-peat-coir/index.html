Coco peat and coir suppliers in Sri Lanka: blocks, grow bags, fibre and husk chips

Sri Lanka exported US$197.54 million of coco peat, fibre pith and moulded products in 2024, and the Export Development Board describes it as the third largest coco peat exporter and the largest supplier of brown coir fibre, at about 100,000 tonnes a year. Exporters supply blocks and bricks, grow bags, discs, cubes and plugs, washed, unwashed or buffered with calcium nitrate, plus coir fibre, twine, geotextiles, mats and husk chips. Many products for Europe need RHP certification.

Why buyers source coco peat and coir from Sri Lanka

  • Sri Lanka is the largest supplier of brown coir fibre, exporting about 100,000 tonnes a year. EDB
  • The Export Development Board describes Sri Lanka as the third largest coco peat exporter. EDB coco peat study
  • Coco peat, fibre pith and moulded products earned US$197.54 million in 2024, up from US$160.72 million in 2023. EDB
  • Moulded coir products for horticulture rose 29% in value to US$182.64 million in January to September 2025, the largest coir category. International Coconut Community
  • Coco peat is supplied washed (low EC), unwashed (high EC) or buffered with calcium nitrate. EDB coco peat study
  • Coir twine comes in 2 and 3 ply from 3.5 mm to 12 mm, and coir geotextiles last up to five years. EDB

Certifications and standards to ask for

  • RHP. Many coco peat products entering Europe, particularly the Netherlands, must meet RHP standards. Ask whether the product and site hold the mark.
  • Lot test reports. EC, pH, moisture and expansion volume for each lot.
  • ISO 9001 and ISO 14001. The Export Development Board says coir manufacturers work to these.
  • Organic. Available from some exporters for coco peat; ask for the certifier.
  • Phytosanitary certificate. Issued by the National Plant Quarantine Service where your country requires it.

Know this before you buy

  • Husk supply is limited: husk chip volumes fell 33% in January to September 2025. International Coconut Community
  • Prices swing: coir and coco peat prices fell by an average of 68% in 2022. EDB coco peat study
  • Coir fibre prices in Sri Lanka rose from the mid US$60s a tonne in early 2024 to about US$124 in October 2025. International Coconut Community
  • Coco peat and moulded product exports fell 10.63% in September 2026 even as husk chips rose, so monthly volumes are uneven. EDB

Questions to ask a Sri Lankan coco peat exporter

  • Is the product washed, unwashed or buffered, and what EC do you guarantee?
  • Does the site hold RHP, if I sell in Europe?
  • What expansion volume and moisture do you guarantee per block?
  • Can you make grow bags to my dimensions and fill?
  • What is your monthly capacity in containers?

Common questions

What forms of coco peat can Sri Lankan suppliers ship?

Compressed blocks, bricks and briquettes, loose coco peat, grow bags, discs, cubes, open tops and nursery plugs, washed, unwashed or buffered with calcium nitrate.

Do Sri Lankan coco peat products meet European requirements?

Many coco peat products entering Europe must meet RHP standards. Ask each supplier whether the product and site hold the RHP mark.

Where does Sri Lankan coco peat go?

In 2022 the largest markets were Mexico, China, the Netherlands, Japan, the US and South Korea.

Which HS codes cover coir products?

Coco peat, pith and fibre are grouped under 5305.00, coir twine and rope under 5607.90, geotextiles under 5311.00 and coconut husk chips under 1404.90.

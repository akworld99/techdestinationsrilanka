Source from Sri Lanka

Desiccated coconut, coconut milk and virgin coconut oil suppliers in Sri Lanka

Sri Lanka's coconut and coconut based exports rose 42.66% to US$1.23 billion in 2025, led by coconut cream, coconut oil and milk powder. The Export Development Board ranks Sri Lanka the fourth largest exporter of coconut products, and in 2024 it exported US$138 million of liquid coconut milk and US$112 million of desiccated coconut. Kernel processors hold ISO 22000, FSSC 22000, HACCP and BRC certificates, and every desiccated coconut shipment carries a Coconut Development Authority certificate. Nut supply has been falling, so build price review clauses into contracts.

Last reviewed 27 October 2026. Sources are linked next to each fact. We do not name buyers.

Why buyers source coconut food products from Sri Lanka

  • The Export Development Board ranks Sri Lanka the fourth largest exporter of coconut products. EDB
  • In 2018 Sri Lanka was the largest exporter of coconut milk, cream and milk powder, and the third largest exporter of desiccated coconut. EDB coconut report
  • Coconut and coconut based exports rose 42.66% to US$1,233.01 million in 2025. EDB via Daily FT
  • Kernel product facilities are certified under ISO 22000, FSSC 22000, HACCP and BRC. EDB
  • Desiccated coconut comes in super fine, fine and medium cuts with moisture held at 2.5% to 3.0%. EDB

Certifications and standards to ask for

  • ISO 22000 or FSSC 22000. Food safety, held across kernel product mills.
  • HACCP and BRC. Ask for current certificates and the latest audit.
  • Coconut Development Authority certificate. Required with every desiccated coconut shipment.
  • Organic. Some farms are organic certified; ask for the scheme and certifier.
  • Halal and kosher. Ask whether the processor holds them for your product.

Know this before you buy

  • Nut output fell to 2,684 million nuts in 2024 from 2,950 million in 2023, tightening raw material supply. Daily FT
  • The International Coconut Community reported Sri Lankan desiccated coconut among the highest priced in the world and expects the market to stay volatile. International Coconut Community
  • Kernel product exports fell 29.15% in September 2026, so monthly volumes can swing sharply. EDB
  • Exporters may import limited quantities of kernel products for re export. If you need 100% Sri Lankan origin, specify it and ask for origin documents. Daily FT

Questions to ask a Sri Lankan coconut processor

  • Which cuts, fat content and moisture levels do you supply?
  • Is all kernel 100% Sri Lankan origin?
  • Which food safety certificates do you hold, and when is the next audit?
  • Do you make private label coconut milk or cream, and in which pack formats?
  • How do you price contracts when nut prices move?

Common questions

Which HS codes apply to desiccated coconut and coconut milk?

Desiccated coconut is 0801.11, coconut oil 1513.11, coconut milk, cream and milk powder 2008.19, and coconut water 2106.90. Confirm the national tariff line in your market with your customs broker.

What food safety certificates do Sri Lankan coconut processors hold?

ISO 22000, FSSC 22000, HACCP and BRC, according to the Export Development Board, and every desiccated coconut shipment carries a Coconut Development Authority certificate.

How large are Sri Lanka's coconut food exports?

In 2024: US$138 million of liquid coconut milk, US$112 million of desiccated coconut, US$83 million of coconut oil, US$57 million of milk powder and US$45 million of cream. All coconut exports reached US$1.23 billion in 2025.

Is supply reliable all year?

Supply follows the nut harvest, which has fallen since 2023. Agree buffer stock and price review clauses with your supplier.

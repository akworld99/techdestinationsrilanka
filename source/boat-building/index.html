Boat builders in Sri Lanka: fibreglass boats, catamarans, workboats and patrol craft

Sri Lankan boatyards build yachts and leisure boats, catamarans, fishing vessels, passenger and crew boats, landing craft, tugs, patrol and rescue craft and pontoons, mostly in fibreglass and composite, with larger vessels in aluminium and steel. Norway bought US$43.1 million of Sri Lankan built boats in 2025, up from US$21 million in 2023. Yards build to Lloyd's Register, DNV, Bureau Veritas and ABS rules, but the Export Development Board names certification as the sector's biggest barrier for leisure boats, so confirm CE or class documents per yard and model.

Why buyers source boats and marine from Sri Lanka

  • Norway bought US$43.1 million of Sri Lankan built boats in 2025, up from US$21 million in 2023. EDB Norway market profile
  • The sector exported more than US$40 million in 2024, with about 76 active boatyards and about 5,000 direct jobs. Board of Investment
  • Vessels are built to Lloyd's Register, DNV, Bureau Veritas and ABS rules. EDB boat building sector document
  • Most hulls are fibreglass and composite; larger vessels are built in aluminium and steel. EDB
  • In September 2026 the government committed to a dedicated boat manufacturing zone. Daily Mirror

Certifications and standards to ask for

  • Classification. Ask which society the vessel is built and surveyed to: Lloyd's Register, DNV, Bureau Veritas or ABS.
  • CE marking. Required for leisure boats sold in the EU under the Recreational Craft Directive 2013/53/EU.
  • ABYC and NMMA. Expected for leisure boats sold in the US.

Know this before you buy

  • The Export Development Board names certification and compliance as the sector's most significant barrier for leisure boats. EDB
  • Exports depend on individual vessel orders and are concentrated in one market, so revenue is lumpy and yard capacity varies. EDB Norway market profile
  • Financing and working capital remain challenges for small and medium builders, which matters for large or long lead orders. EDB

Questions to ask a Sri Lankan boat builder

  • Which vessels of this type and size have you delivered, and to whom can we speak?
  • Which classification society will survey the build?
  • Can you deliver CE or ABYC documentation for this model?
  • What is the build slot and the payment schedule?
  • How do you handle warranty and after sales support abroad?

Common questions

What boats are built in Sri Lanka for export?

Yachts and leisure boats, catamarans, fishing vessels, passenger and crew boats, landing craft, tugs, patrol and rescue craft, pontoons and floating restaurants.

Who buys Sri Lankan boats?

Norway is the largest market, at US$43.1 million in 2025, followed by the Maldives. France, India, Spain, the US and the Netherlands are also named.

Are Sri Lankan boats classed and CE marked?

Yards build to Lloyd's Register, DNV, Bureau Veritas and ABS rules. EU leisure boats need CE marking, so confirm the documents per yard and model.

Tender Radar: international tender alerts on WhatsApp

International tenders for what you make, found for you and sent to your WhatsApp. Each alert says what is being bought, by whom, by when and what the tender will ask you for, with our read on whether it is worth your time.

Start on WhatsAppSet up with the form

Why exporters miss tenders they could win

  • Tenders are spread across national e-procurement portals, utility tender boards, development bank sites and UN procurement systems. Nobody on a factory floor has time to check them all.
  • Notices are written in procurement language, often in long documents, and the clause that rules you out can sit on page 40.
  • By the time a tender reaches you through an agent there are days left, not weeks, and no time to get type tests, guarantees and certificates in order.

How it works

  1. Set your profile

    Products, ratings and sizes, the markets you can deliver to, the certificates and type tests you hold, and the smallest contract worth your time.

  2. We scan the sources

    Utility and government tender portals, development bank and UN procurement notices, and supplier requests from contractors, every working day.

  3. Matches reach your WhatsApp

    Only tenders that fit your profile, each with the buyer, quantity, deadline and key requirements, plus deadline reminders and a weekly digest.

  4. Reply to act

    Reply BID for a go or no go review, DOCS for the document checklist, or STOP to pause alerts at any time.

Where the tenders come from

  • UN agencies publish procurement on the UN Global Marketplace. Basic supplier registration asks only for general company information. UNGM
  • UN Secretariat awards above US$500,000 need Level 2 supplier registration; Basic level covers awards below US$150,000. UN Procurement Division
  • The World Bank counts about 40,000 procurement opportunities a year on the projects it finances. World Bank
  • Bidding documents for World Bank financed goods admit firms from all countries. Utility bidding document under World Bank rules
  • Asian Development Bank financed contracts are open to suppliers from member countries; Sri Lanka has been a member since 1966. Asian Development Bank
  • Sri Lanka is not an African Development Bank member, so its exporters target contracts financed through the African Development Fund window, which is open to any country. African Development Bank
  • UN agencies buy health, nutrition, water and sanitation and education supplies, with suppliers registering through the UN Global Marketplace. UN supply division page
  • National utilities and government buyers publish their own tenders on national e-procurement systems. Eligibility for foreign firms varies, so read the clause in each notice. National e-procurement notice

What a tender will ask you for

  • A bid security, for example 2% of the bid price. Utility transformer tender
  • The bid security is usually a bank guarantee, or a bid securing declaration where the bid data sheet allows it. World Bank standard bidding document
  • A performance security of 10% of the contract price if you win. UN power transformer tender
  • A manufacturer's authorisation if the bidder does not make the goods itself. World Bank standard bidding document
  • Transformer type tests to IEC 60076, with reports often no older than five years. Utility transformer specification
  • Cables to IEC 60502 with conductors to IEC 60228. Utility cable tender
  • Switchgear panels with valid type test certificates to IEC 61439, and ISO 9001 for the factory. Switchgear prequalification
  • Past supply records, for example two supplies of similar items in three years, and a minimum average turnover. Utility transformer tender
  • Guaranteed technical particulars with binding values, such as losses with no positive tolerance. Utility transformer tender
  • Factory acceptance tests witnessed by the buyer's engineers. Pacific utility transformer tender
  • For uniforms: manufacturer status or a letter from the manufacturer, pre production samples and a documented quality system. UN uniforms tender
  • For medical gloves: EN 455 parts 1 to 4, an AQL of 1.5 for holes, and test reports from ISO/IEC 17025 accredited labs. Public health glove specification

Gaps here are what sink first bids. Our bid support starts by checking your documents against the tender before you spend time on the price.

Common questions

Which tenders does Tender Radar cover?

International tenders for electrical and engineering equipment, pumps and machinery, uniforms, gloves and medical rubber products, packaging and boats, from utilities, governments, development bank financed projects, UN agencies and contractors.

Can Sri Lankan companies bid on World Bank, ADB and UN tenders?

In most cases, yes. World Bank financed contracts are open to firms from all countries, Asian Development Bank financed contracts to member countries including Sri Lanka, and UN agencies buy through the UN Global Marketplace. African Development Bank contracts are for members, but African Development Fund financed contracts are open to any country.

Do you bid for us?

No. You stay the bidder and every document goes out under your name. Our bid desk helps you read the tender, check your documents and prepare the bid.

Why WhatsApp?

Because tender deadlines are short and an alert needs to be seen the same day. You opt in to alerts, as WhatsApp's business rules require, and you can pause them any time by replying STOP. The weekly digest can come by email too.

How quickly do alerts arrive?

We check sources every working day and send a match the day we find it, with a reminder before the deadline.

What does a typical tender ask for?

Usually a bid security, a performance security if you win, the manufacturer's authorisation, type test reports to the relevant IEC standard, ISO 9001, past supply records and minimum turnover. The details are in each tender, and Radar flags them in every alert.

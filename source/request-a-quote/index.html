What happens next

  1. Tell us the spec

    Product, grade, quantity, packing, destination port, the certificates you need and when you need the first shipment.

  2. We shortlist exporters

    We match you with Sri Lankan exporters who make that product and already ship to markets like yours, and check them before we introduce them.

  3. You deal direct

    Quotes and samples come from the exporters themselves and you choose. We stay on hand for the factory visit, the first order and the first shipment.

What we check before an introduction

  • The company is registered in Sri Lanka and listed as an exporter, through the Export Development Board directory or a chamber of commerce.
  • The certificates you asked for exist, are current and cover the site that will make your order.
  • It has shipped this product to your region before, and can show export documents or a buyer reference.
  • It has the capacity for your volume and your delivery dates.
  • A video call or a visit to the factory, on request, before you pay a deposit.

Common questions

Do you sell the products yourselves?

No. We are not a trading company. Quotes, contracts, payment and shipping are agreed between you and the exporter. We find and check the exporters and stay on hand until your first shipment.

How do you choose which exporters to introduce?

By the product and grade you need, the certificates you ask for, the markets they already ship to and their capacity for your volume. We check registration and certificates before we introduce anyone.

Can I get samples before I order?

Yes. Ask for samples before a first order, and agree in writing what the sample has to prove, such as grade, moisture or dimensions, so the order can be checked against it.

What if I only need a small quantity?

Tell us anyway. Minimum orders vary by product and exporter, and some exporters accept a trial order before a full container.

Which markets do Sri Lankan exporters ship to?

In 2025 the largest markets for Sri Lankan goods were the US, India, the UK, Germany, Italy and the Netherlands, according to the Export Development Board. Many exporters also ship to the Gulf, Japan, China, Australia and Turkey.

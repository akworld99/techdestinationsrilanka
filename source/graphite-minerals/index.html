Sri Lanka vein graphite, ilmenite and quartz: mineral suppliers and export rules

Sri Lanka is the only country that commercially produces natural crystalline vein graphite, graded above 90% carbon, according to the Export Development Board. It also exports ilmenite, rutile and zircon from the Pulmoddai beach sand deposit, high purity vein quartz and mica. Volumes are small: about 4,000 tonnes of vein graphite in 2019 by US Geological Survey estimates. Raw and semi finished minerals need an export licence from the Geological Survey and Mines Bureau.

Why buyers source graphite and minerals from Sri Lanka

  • Sri Lanka is the only country that commercially produces natural crystalline vein graphite. EDB
  • Sri Lankan vein graphite is graded above 90% carbon. EDB
  • Vein graphite output was about 4,000 tonnes in 2019, and ilmenite and leucoxene 43,779 tonnes. US Geological Survey
  • The Pulmoddai beach sand deposit north of Trincomalee is the main mineral sand source, and ilmenite is its main mineral. EDB
  • High purity vein quartz with more than 98% silica is found in many districts, and white silica sand above 98% in Puttalam. EDB

Certifications and standards to ask for

  • GSMB export licence. Minerals in raw and semi finished form need an export licence from the Geological Survey and Mines Bureau.
  • Export cess. Raw forms carry an export cess, for example Rs 4,400 a tonne on raw vein quartz, so check whether quotes include it.
  • Certificate of analysis. We found no national standard, so set carbon content, particle size and moisture in the contract and test with an independent lab.

Know this before you buy

  • Graphite export volumes have fallen since 2015 while unit prices rose, according to a 2025 University of Moratuwa study of UN trade data. University of Moratuwa
  • A parliamentary inquiry in September 2025 found clear quartz being exported illegally and royalty collection failures, so do full documentary checks. Ada Derana

Questions to ask a Sri Lankan mineral exporter

  • Do you hold a Geological Survey and Mines Bureau export licence for this mineral?
  • Which mine or deposit does the material come from?
  • Can you supply an independent certificate of analysis for each lot?
  • What monthly volume can you commit to, and at what particle sizes?
  • Does your price include the export cess?

Common questions

What is Sri Lankan vein graphite?

Natural crystalline graphite found in veins, which Sri Lanka is the only country to produce commercially. It is graded above 90% carbon and used in lubricants, carbon brushes, brake pads and refractories.

How much graphite does Sri Lanka produce?

About 4,000 tonnes of vein graphite in 2019 by US Geological Survey estimates, so confirm capacity before you contract.

Can minerals be exported raw?

Only under a Geological Survey and Mines Bureau export licence, and raw forms carry an export cess.

Where do Sri Lankan minerals go?

The Export Development Board names the UK, Eastern and Western Europe, the US, India, Pakistan and China as the main markets.

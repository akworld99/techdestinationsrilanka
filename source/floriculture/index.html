Source from Sri Lanka

Cut foliage, live plant and aquarium plant exporters in Sri Lanka

Sri Lanka exported US$13.67 million of cut flowers and foliage in 2024, mostly foliage such as dracaena and cordyline (US$11.69 million). Exporters also supply aquarium plants including Cryptocoryne, Anubias and Echinodorus. Europe takes 60% of floriculture exports, led by the Netherlands. The main exporters grow in their own nurseries and hold GLOBALG.A.P., MPS and Fair Trade certificates. Units are small, so large programmes may need several growers.

Last reviewed 27 October 2026. Sources are linked next to each fact. We do not name buyers.

Why buyers source cut foliage and live plants from Sri Lanka

  • Europe takes 60% of Sri Lankan floriculture exports. EDB
  • Cut flowers and foliage exports were US$13.67 million in 2024, of which foliage was US$11.69 million. EDB
  • Export companies hold GLOBALG.A.P., MPS and Fair Trade certifications. EDB floriculture brochure
  • All major exporters are producer exporters running their own nurseries. EDB
  • Aquarium plant exports include Cryptocoryne, Echinodorus, Anubias, Lagenandra and Aponogeton. EDB

Certifications and standards to ask for

  • Phytosanitary certificate. Issued after plant quarantine checks by the National Plant Quarantine Service.
  • GLOBALG.A.P., MPS and Fair Trade. Held by export companies; ask for the current certificate.
  • CITES. Needed for listed species, including some orchids.

Know this before you buy

  • Cold storage at the airport, direct flights to the Netherlands and air freight rates are the sector's main constraints. EDB floriculture report
  • Cut flower and foliage exports fell from US$15.01 million in 2023 to US$13.67 million in 2024. EDB

Questions to ask a Sri Lankan floriculture exporter

  • Do you grow in your own nurseries, and what is your weekly volume per variety?
  • Which certificates do you hold: GLOBALG.A.P., MPS, Fair Trade?
  • How do you pack and keep the cold chain to the airport?
  • Does any variety need CITES papers or an import permit in my country?
  • Can you grow to my specification or contract grow new varieties?

Common questions

What floriculture products does Sri Lanka export most?

Cut foliage, at US$11.69 million of the US$13.67 million cut flowers and foliage total in 2024, plus live plants and aquarium plants.

Which markets buy Sri Lankan foliage?

The Netherlands, Japan, Saudi Arabia, Australia and the UK. Europe takes 60% of floriculture exports.

Which aquarium plants are available?

Cryptocoryne, Echinodorus, Anubias, Lagenandra and Aponogeton, grown for export in supervised nurseries.

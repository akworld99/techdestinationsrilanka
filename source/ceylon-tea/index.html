Source from Sri Lanka

Ceylon tea suppliers and exporters in Sri Lanka: bulk, packets and private label

Sri Lanka exported US$1.51 billion of tea in 2025, about 257 million kilograms, and supplied 13% of the world's tea exports in 2024. Ceylon tea is grown in seven regions and graded by elevation, and about 93% is made by orthodox methods. Most of it is sold through the Colombo Tea Auction, which runs every Tuesday and Wednesday. Exporters supply bulk tea, packets, tea bags, green, white and instant tea, and 59% of exports in 2025 were value added. Only 100% Ceylon tea packed in Sri Lanka may carry the Sri Lanka Tea Board's Lion logo.

Last reviewed 27 October 2026. Sources are linked next to each fact. We do not name buyers.

Why buyers source Ceylon tea from Sri Lanka

  • Sri Lanka supplied 13% of global tea exports in 2024. EDB
  • Tea exports reached 257.44 million kg in 2025, worth US$1.51 billion at an average FOB of US$5.85 a kg. Daily FT
  • The Colombo Tea Auction runs every Tuesday and Wednesday and sells about 6.5 million kg a week. EDB
  • Ceylon tea is grown in seven regions, including Nuwara Eliya, Uva and Dimbula, and graded by elevation. EDB
  • About 93% of Ceylon tea is made by orthodox and artisanal methods, handpicked as two leaves and a bud. EDB
  • Value added segments reached 59% of tea exports in 2025, an 11 year high. Daily FT

Certifications and standards to ask for

  • Lion logo. Owned by the Sri Lanka Tea Board; only for 100% Ceylon tea packed in Sri Lanka by a licensed packer. Blends do not qualify.
  • ISO 3720. The minimum product standard for Ceylon black tea.
  • ISO 22000. Food safety; the Export Development Board says Ceylon tea meets the ISO 22000 series and EU health and safety rules.
  • Organic. EU organic, USDA NOP, JAS and NASAA certification is available in the industry.
  • Rainforest Alliance, Fairtrade and Ethical Tea Partnership. Listed as industry partnerships; check the estate or factory you buy from.

Know this before you buy

  • Tea earnings fell 6.83% in January to September 2026, with bulk tea down 11.13%; tea bags rose 53.39%. EDB via Newswire
  • 2024 production was 262 million kg, below the industry's stated capacity, so supply of some grades is tight. Daily FT
  • If you blend or pack outside Sri Lanka, you cannot use the Lion logo. EDB

Questions to ask a Sri Lankan tea exporter

  • Which regions and elevations do you buy from, and can you match my blend?
  • Do you buy at the Colombo auction, and how do you price forward contracts?
  • Are you a licensed packer for the Lion logo?
  • Which certificates do you hold: ISO 22000, organic, Rainforest Alliance?
  • What is your minimum order for private label packs or tea bags?

Common questions

What does the Lion logo on Ceylon tea guarantee?

The Sri Lanka Tea Board owns it. It can only appear on packs of 100% pure Ceylon tea, packed in Sri Lanka by a licensed packer and meeting Tea Board quality standards. A blend does not qualify, even at 95% Ceylon tea.

In what formats can I buy Ceylon tea?

Bulk tea, tea packets, tea bags and instant tea, in black, green, white and oolong, with organic, decaffeinated and flavoured versions. In 2025 packets were about 45% of export volume and bulk tea 106.8 million kg.

How is Ceylon tea traded?

Most tea is sold at the Colombo Tea Auction every Tuesday and Wednesday, about 6.5 million kg a week. Exporters buy at auction and sell to overseas buyers in bulk, packed or private label form.

Which countries buy the most Ceylon tea?

By volume in 2025: Iraq, Russia, Turkiye, Libya and the UAE, followed by Chile, China, Iran, Azerbaijan and Saudi Arabia. The Middle East and North Africa took half of exports in 2024.

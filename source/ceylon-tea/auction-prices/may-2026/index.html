Common questions

What was the average Colombo tea auction price in May 2026?

In May 2026 the Colombo tea auction held 4 sales and sold 20.23 million kg at an average of Rs 1,200.69 a kilo, weighted by quantity. That is up 3.2% on May 2025 (Rs 1,163.69). Low Grown teas averaged Rs 1,289.49, High Grown Rs 1,117.94 and Medium Grown Rs 1,015.28.

How did tea prices in May 2026 compare with May 2025?

The all teas average was Rs 1,200.69 a kilo against Rs 1,163.69, up 3.2%. High Grown teas were up 9.2%. Medium Grown teas were down 1.7%. Low Grown teas were up 2.7%.

What was the green leaf price in May 2026?

The rate each factory pays depends on its own net sale average. As an estimate at the elevation average, the formula gives about Low Grown Rs 195, High Grown Rs 169, Medium Grown Rs 153 a kilo of green leaf. Check the rate your factory announces, or work it out for your factory with the green leaf price calculator.

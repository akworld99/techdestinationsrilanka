What moves Colombo prices

A study of 105 weekly broker reports from November 2023 to March 2026 found market and structural factors, such as grade and catalogue, drive prices far more than weather. Weather is a secondary, lagged signal: Low Grown prices responded to rain and sunshine one to three weeks earlier, while High Grown prices showed no lasting weather effect (study, August 2026).

Common questions

What is the average price at the Colombo tea auction?

The average price at the Colombo tea auction sale of 5 August 2026 was Rs 1,181.35 a kilo, across 4.77 million kg. Low Grown teas averaged Rs 1,286.99, High Grown Rs 1,073.04 and Medium Grown Rs 940.59. Across the 30 sales of 2026 to that date the average was Rs 1,179.65, up 0.9% on the same period of 2025.

When is the Colombo tea auction held?

Every week, usually over two days on Tuesday and Wednesday, with occasional one day or rescheduled sales around holidays. Each sale offers around 5 to 6 million kg of tea in catalogues from the licensed brokers.

What are High, Medium and Low Grown teas?

Ceylon tea is grouped by the elevation of the estate where it is grown. The three groups sell at different prices: at the latest sale Low Grown teas averaged Rs 1,287 a kilo against Rs 941 for Medium Grown. Low Grown teas were 63% of the quantity sold at auction in 2026.

Where do these figures come from?

From the weekly tea auction quantities and averages that Forbes & Walker Tea Brokers publish. We check that every sale adds up before we store it and add each new sale when the broker publishes it. Monthly averages are our calculation, weighted by the quantity sold at each sale. The Sri Lanka Tea Board publishes the official monthly national sale average, which is calculated differently and can differ by 1 to 2%.

Low Grown tea prices at the Colombo auction

Low Grown teas averaged Rs 1,266.82 a kilo at the Colombo tea auction sale of 4 November 2026. Over the past 52 weeks the weekly Low Grown average ranged from Rs 1,154.30 (4 Mar 2026) to Rs 1,366.20 (30 Sep 2026). In 2026 to date Low Grown teas averaged Rs 1,272.64, up 2.0% on the same period of 2025, and made up 64% of the quantity sold.

Figures to the sale of 4 November 2026, from Forbes & Walker Tea Brokers. We add each sale when the broker publishes it. How we check

What Low Grown teas are

Ceylon teas grown from sea level to 600 m (Tea Exporters Association). Low grown teas are mainly popular in Western Asia and the Middle East (Export Development Board).

Green leaf rate at the Low Grown average

At the October 2026 Low Grown average of Rs 1,318.31, the green leaf formula gives about Rs 199.19 a kilo of leaf. Your factory's rate depends on its own net sale average: green leaf price calculator.

What is the Low Grown tea price at the Colombo auction?

What was the highest Low Grown tea price in the past year?

The highest weekly Low Grown average in the 52 weeks to 4 Nov 2026 was Rs 1,366.20 a kilo, at the sale of 30 September 2026. The lowest was Rs 1,154.30, on 4 March 2026.

The elevation average is filled in with our October 2026 Colombo auction average for that elevation, weighted by quantity sold, from Forbes & Walker Tea Brokers' weekly figures (see the prices). Use the Tea Board's official figure for the month if you have it; it can differ by up to about 2%. The scenarios hold the elevation average fixed.

The formula

As documented by the Tea Research Institute in 2003, with NSA the factory's net sale average and EA the elevation average:

  • If NSA is at or below EA: leaf rate = out-turn × 68% × NSA
  • If NSA is above EA: leaf rate = out-turn × (68% × EA + 50% × (NSA − EA))
  • Out-turn: 22.22 kg of made tea from 100 kg of green leaf

Change the settings above if the Tea Commissioner's Division has revised any of these.

Common questions

How is the green leaf price calculated in Sri Lanka?

Bought-leaf factories pay for green leaf under a statutory formula tied to the factory's net sale average at auction. As documented by the Tea Research Institute, the supplier receives 68% of the net sale average when it is at or below the elevation average, or 68% of the elevation average plus 50% of the excess when it is above, converted at a fixed out-turn of 22.22 kg of made tea per 100 kg of green leaf.

What is a factory's net sale average?

The average price per kilo that a factory's made tea fetched at the Colombo auction over the month.

Why does the elevation average matter?

Above the elevation average the supplier's share of the extra drops from 68% to 50%, so a factory that sells above its elevation's average keeps more of that premium.

Is this the official green leaf price?

No. It is an estimate from the formula as last documented. The Tea Commissioner's Division of the Sri Lanka Tea Board sets and enforces the rules, so confirm current parameters with it before you rely on a figure.

What is the green leaf price this month?

It depends on each factory's net sale average. As an estimate at the October 2026 elevation averages, the formula gives about Rs 199 for Low Grown, Rs 175 for High Grown, Rs 152 for Medium Grown a kilo of green leaf.

What is dalu mila?

Dalu mila (දළු මිල in Sinhala) is the price a factory pays for green tea leaf, the same green leaf rate this calculator works out. In Tamil it is கொழுந்து விலை (kolunthu vilai).

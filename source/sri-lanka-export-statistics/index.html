Sri Lanka export statistics: 2025 and 2026, by product and by country

Sri Lanka exported US$17.25 billion of goods and services in 2025, up 5.6%: US$13.58 billion of goods and US$3.67 billion of services. From January to September 2026 exports reached US$13.61 billion, up 5.04%, with goods at US$10.76 billion, up 5.35%. The largest export is apparel and textiles, at US$4.91 billion in 2025, followed by tea, coconut products and rubber products. The fastest growing in 2026 are electrical and electronic components, up 61%, and spices and essential oils, up 30.5%. The United States is the largest market, at about a fifth of goods exports, followed by India and the United Kingdom.

What changed in 2026

  • Electrical and electronic components rose 61.05% to US$508.86 million, led by insulated wires and cables, up 82.59%, and transformers, up 33.46%. EDB, January to September 2026
  • Spices and essential oils rose 30.53%: pepper up 61.45% to US$136.26 million and cinnamon up 31.03% to US$221.56 million. EDB, January to September 2026
  • Food and beverages rose 20.90%, with processed food up 34.19% and non-alcoholic beverages up 46.78%. EDB, January to September 2026
  • Tea fell 6.83% overall, but tea bags rose 53.39% and tea exports to Türkiye rose 126.51%. EDB, January to September 2026
  • Activated carbon exports to China rose 78.33%. EDB, January to September 2026
  • Apparel fell 5.68%, with apparel to the US down 3.83%, the EU down 5.54% and the UK down 10.41%. EDB, January to September 2026
  • September 2026 alone was strong: goods exports rose 21.66% to US$1.39 billion. EDB, September 2026

Common questions

What are Sri Lanka's main exports?

Apparel and textiles (US$4.91 billion in 2025), tea (US$1.51 billion), coconut products (US$1.23 billion), rubber products (US$946 million), food and beverages (US$584 million), spices and essential oils (US$450 million), electrical and electronic components (US$438 million), gems and jewellery and seafood. In services, logistics brought in US$1.85 billion and IT and business process services US$1.64 billion.

How much did Sri Lanka export in 2025?

US$17.25 billion of goods and services, up 5.6% on 2024. Goods were US$13.58 billion, up 6.32%, and services US$3.67 billion, up 2.79%, according to the Export Development Board.

What is Sri Lanka's export income in 2026?

From January to September 2026 Sri Lanka earned US$13.61 billion from exports, up 5.04% on the same months of 2025. Goods were US$10.76 billion, up 5.35%, and services an estimated US$2.85 billion, up 3.88%.

Which countries buy the most from Sri Lanka?

The United States, at US$3.0 billion in 2025 and about a fifth of goods exports, then India (US$1.04 billion), the United Kingdom (US$929 million), Germany, Italy and the Netherlands. In January to September 2026 China, the UAE and Türkiye each took more than US$200 million, and Türkiye grew fastest, up 94%.

Which Sri Lankan exports are growing fastest?

In January to September 2026: electrical and electronic components, up 61.05%, spices and essential oils, up 30.53%, and food and beverages, up 20.90%. Within those, pepper rose 61%, insulated cables 83% and processed food 34%.

Where do these figures come from?

From the Export Development Board's monthly export releases, linked on this page. They are provisional and can be revised, and the services figures are estimates.

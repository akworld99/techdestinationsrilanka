Tender bid support and export consultancy for Sri Lankan manufacturers

We help Sri Lankan manufacturers bid for international tenders and build the export business around them. On a live tender we read the documents, tell you honestly whether to bid, build the compliance matrix, and prepare the technical, commercial and document packs with your team until the bid is submitted. Around the bid, we help with market selection, vendor registration, certification roadmaps and buyer outreach.

What we do on a bid

  1. Read the tender

    Eligibility, qualification criteria, evaluation method, lots, deadlines and the clauses that can rule you out.

  2. Go or no go

    A short written view on fit, gaps and the cost of bidding, so you only bid where you can win.

  3. Ask before the deadline

    Clarification questions drafted for you to send within the buyer's clarification window.

  4. Compliance matrix

    Every requirement mapped to your evidence, clause by clause, with gaps flagged early.

  5. Technical submission

    Guaranteed technical particulars, data sheets, drawings, type test reports and a clear list of deviations.

  6. Commercial submission

    Price schedules, delivery terms, freight and insurance, currency and payment terms, and the bid security with your bank.

  7. Documents and forms

    Manufacturer's authorisation, experience certificates, financial statements, ISO certificates, and notarisation or legalisation where asked.

  8. Submit and follow up

    Final checks before you upload or courier the bid, then help with clarifications during evaluation and the performance security if you win.

Export consultancy around the bid

  • Export readiness review. Where your product, documents and certificates stand against what buyers in your target markets ask for.
  • Certification and type testing roadmap. Which certificates and type tests open which tenders and markets, and in what order to get them.
  • Market selection. Where your product and price can win, using trade data and tender history.
  • Vendor registration. Supplier registration with utilities, contractors, UN procurement and e-procurement portals in your target markets.
  • Agents and distributors. Finding and checking local partners where a tender or a market needs one.
  • Buyer facing marketing. A website, catalogue and LinkedIn presence a foreign procurement team will trust, and outreach to buyers in your target markets.

How we work with you

  • You stay the bidder. Every document goes out under your name and your signature.
  • Confidential by default. We sign a non disclosure agreement before you share prices or drawings.
  • One bid per tender. Radar alerts go to every exporter they match, but on any one tender our bid desk prepares only one bid.
  • Honest calls. If we think a tender is not winnable, we say so before you spend money on it.

Common questions

Do you write the whole bid for us?

We can, working from your data, prices and documents. Most manufacturers keep pricing in house and use us for reading the tender, the compliance matrix, the technical pack and the final checks.

What is a go or no go review?

A short written assessment of a tender before you commit: whether you meet the eligibility and qualification criteria, which documents you are missing, how bids will be evaluated, and what bidding will cost you in time, tests and bank guarantees.

Can you help if we have never exported?

Yes, but start with an export readiness review. Many tenders ask for past supply records and type tests, so the first step may be a smaller tender, a contractor's approved supplier list or a distributor rather than a large utility bid.

Which tenders do you support?

Mainly electrical and engineering equipment, uniforms and apparel, rubber and medical supplies, packaging and boats, from utilities, governments, development bank funded projects, UN agencies and contractors.

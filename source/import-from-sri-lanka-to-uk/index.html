Import from Sri Lanka to the UK: what sells, duty access and import rules

The UK bought US$929 million of goods from Sri Lanka in 2025, mostly clothing, followed by coconut milk and rubber gloves. Sri Lanka is in the Enhanced Preferences tier of the UK's Developing Countries Trading Scheme, which gives zero duty on more than 7,000 tariff lines for goods that meet its rules of origin. Animal products and high risk food must be pre-notified on IPAFFS before arrival, and wild caught fish needs a catch certificate. Exports to the UK fell 9.33% in January to September 2026, to US$654 million.

Duty and trade access

  • Sri Lanka is in the Enhanced Preferences tier of the UK Developing Countries Trading Scheme, with zero duty on more than 7,000 tariff lines. EDB, UK DCTS profile
  • The UK has relaxed DCTS rules of origin for Sri Lankan garments (HS chapters 61 and 62), so fabric and other inputs can come from any country without losing tariff free access, as announced in August 2025. Ada Derana
  • UK buyers took US$654.36 million of Sri Lankan goods in January to September 2026, down 9.33%, and apparel to the UK fell 10.41%. EDB, January to September 2026

Import rules to check in the UK

  • Products of animal origin, and high risk food not of animal origin, must be pre-notified on IPAFFS at least one working day before arrival. Dover Port Health Authority
  • Fish and fishery products under CN 03, 1604 or 1605 with more than 20% wild caught content need an IUU catch certificate, plus a processing statement if processed outside the flag state. Dover Port Health Authority

Common questions

Is there import duty from Sri Lanka to the UK?

Many Sri Lankan goods enter at zero duty. Sri Lanka is in the Enhanced Preferences tier of the UK Developing Countries Trading Scheme, which covers more than 7,000 tariff lines, provided the goods meet the scheme's rules of origin. Import VAT still applies.

What does the UK import from Sri Lanka?

Mostly clothing: women's and men's outerwear, undergarments, babies' garments and T-shirts. Coconut milk, activewear, rubber gloves, knitwear and textile gloves follow, according to the EDB's 2025 brief on the UK.

Do I need to pre-notify food imports from Sri Lanka to the UK?

Products of animal origin and high risk food not of animal origin must be pre-notified on IPAFFS at least one working day before arrival. Wild caught fish also needs a catch certificate.

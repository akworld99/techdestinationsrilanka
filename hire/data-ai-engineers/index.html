Hire in Sri Lanka

Hire data and AI engineers in Sri Lanka

You can hire data and AI engineers in Sri Lanka directly, through an employer of record, or as a dedicated team from a Sri Lankan company. A data engineer in Colombo earns a median of about LKR 215,000 a month (about USD 650), and a senior data engineer about LKR 364,000, according to self reported Glassdoor data for 2026. With statutory employer contributions that is roughly USD 9,000 a year at the median. Strong people at export companies earn more.

Last reviewed 10 October 2026. Dollar figures use LKR 331 per dollar.

Common questions

How much do data and AI engineers earn in Sri Lanka?

Self reported Glassdoor data for Colombo in 2026 shows a median of about LKR 215,000 a month (about USD 650) for a data engineer. Export focused employers pay above the median.

Which companies employ data and AI engineers in Sri Lanka?

Global companies with their own centres here, such as IFS, Sysco, Synopsys, Zebra Technologies and EY, alongside Sri Lankan product and services companies.

Do I need a local company to hire data and AI engineers in Sri Lanka?

No. You can hire through an employer of record, contract individuals, or take a dedicated team from a Sri Lankan company. Your own entity makes sense once the team passes a few dozen people.

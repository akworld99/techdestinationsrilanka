Hire IFS consultants in Sri Lanka

You can hire IFS consultants in Sri Lanka directly, through an IFS delivery company, or under a managed support contract. Colombo has the deepest IFS talent pool outside Sweden: IFS has operated there since 1997 and does about 60% of its global product development and about 80% of its global product support from Sri Lanka. ERP consultants in Colombo earn a median of about USD 510 a month; experienced IFS Cloud consultants earn well above that.

Why Sri Lanka for IFS talent

  • IFS is built here. Colombo hosts IFS's largest R&D centre outside Sweden. In 2023 the Sri Lankan team was about 40% of IFS's global workforce, and it nearly tripled between 2017 and 2022 (IFS).
  • Support and consulting too, not only code. IFS runs about 80% of its global product support from Sri Lanka, along with cloud operations and a consulting business unit (IFS). Chief executive Mark Moffat has called Sri Lanka the company's "engine" (Daily FT).
  • An ecosystem around it. People trained at IFS now work at IFS partners and delivery companies in Colombo that implement and support IFS for customers in the Nordics, the UK, Europe, the US and Asia.
  • Hours that suit IFS markets. UTC+5:30 gives several shared hours with the Nordics, the UK and Central Europe every working day.

How to hire IFS consultants in Sri Lanka

  1. Map the modules and versions

    List your IFS version (IFS Applications 8, 9, 10 or IFS Cloud), the modules in use, your integrations and the customisations you carry.

  2. Decide what you need

    Functional consultants for process and configuration, technical consultants for development and integrations, or a support team for day to day tickets.

  3. Choose the model

    Direct hires through an employer of record, staff augmentation from an IFS delivery company, a fixed scope project team, or an application managed services contract.

  4. Test on your own scenarios

    Ask candidates to walk through a real process from your business, such as a month end close or a work order, in the IFS module concerned.

  5. Set the overlap and handover

    Agree shared hours with your team, a ticket process and documentation standards before the first sprint or support month starts.

Common questions

Why hire IFS consultants from Sri Lanka?

Because that is where much of IFS itself is built and supported. IFS has operated in Sri Lanka since 1997, has its largest R&D centre outside Sweden in Colombo, and does about 60% of its global product development and about 80% of its global product support there. That has produced the deepest pool of IFS trained people anywhere outside Sweden.

How many people does IFS employ in Sri Lanka?

Over 2,500 in early 2024, with plans to reach about 3,500, according to IFS chief executive Mark Moffat speaking to the Daily FT. In 2023 IFS said its Sri Lankan team was about 40% of its global workforce.

How much does an IFS consultant cost in Sri Lanka?

There is no public salary data for IFS consultants specifically. ERP consultants in Colombo earn a median of about LKR 169,000 a month (about USD 510) and SAP consultants about LKR 219,000 (about USD 660), on self reported Glassdoor data for 2026. Senior and lead IFS consultants with IFS Cloud experience earn well above these medians.

Can Sri Lankan IFS consultants support customers in Europe and the US?

Yes. IFS's own global support runs largely from Colombo. The working day overlaps Europe in the afternoon and the Nordics and UK for several hours. US coverage is usually handled with a later shift or a support rota.

What IFS work can be done from Sri Lanka?

Most of it: configuration, development, integrations, reports, data migration, testing, upgrades to IFS Cloud and application managed support. Workshops with your process owners and go-live weeks are often done on site or with a local lead.

Should I hire IFS consultants directly or through a partner?

For one or two long term people, hire directly through an employer of record. For a project or an upgrade, a Sri Lankan IFS delivery team brings ready made functional and technical cover. For ongoing support, an application managed services contract is usually cheaper than building your own bench.

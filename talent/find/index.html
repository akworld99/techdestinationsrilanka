Common questions

Do I need a company in Sri Lanka to hire there?

No. You can hire through an employer of record, contract individuals, or take a dedicated team from a Sri Lankan company.

What roles can I hire in Sri Lanka?

Software engineers, QA, DevOps and cloud, data and AI, designers and product managers, ERP consultants, cybersecurity engineers, and finance and accounting professionals.

What does a Sri Lankan software engineer cost?

A median of about LKR 177,000 a month, roughly USD 535, on self reported 2026 Glassdoor data, plus 15% statutory employer contributions. Strong engineers at export companies earn more.

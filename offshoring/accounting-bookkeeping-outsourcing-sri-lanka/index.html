Accounting and bookkeeping outsourcing to Sri Lanka

Sri Lanka has one of the deepest pools of professionally qualified accountants of any offshore destination: CA Sri Lanka alone has more than 9,000 members and 40,000 students, and around 15,000 people are CIMA students or members. Global banks and exchanges run finance operations in Colombo, including HSBC since 2004 and the London Stock Exchange Group, whose Colombo hubs employ more than 1,600 people. Accounting firms and companies in the UK, Australia, the US and the Gulf outsource bookkeeping, accounts payable and receivable, reconciliations, month-end close and management reporting to Sri Lankan teams.

AI: what is being automated, and what is not

Invoice capture, AP keying, bank feeds and routine journals are being automated by OCR and AI accounting tools, and a 2025 Institute of Policy Studies study names clerical roles as the most exposed in Sri Lanka. The work worth outsourcing now is the judgement layer: owning the close, handling exceptions, writing management commentary, reviewing what the automation produces and keeping controls tight. That is where qualified Sri Lankan accountants add value, so buy accountants, not data entry.

What is accounting outsourcing?

Accounting outsourcing means handing day to day finance work, such as bookkeeping, accounts payable and receivable, reconciliations and month-end close, to an external team that works in your systems under your controls. Offshore accounting does the same with a team in another country, usually to reach qualified people at lower cost and to keep work moving overnight.

How to outsource accounting and bookkeeping to Sri Lanka, step by step

  1. Pick one process

    Choose a single, well documented process with clear volumes and quality measures, such as accounts payable or tier two support.

  2. Decide the model

    A managed provider runs it for you; a dedicated team works only for you through a staffing provider or employer of record; your own centre comes later.

  3. Shortlist two or three providers

    Ask each the questions below, check references in your country and meet the people who would do the work.

  4. Run a paid pilot

    Four to eight weeks with clear targets, with your team reviewing output daily at first.

  5. Agree the contract

    Service levels, data protection, IP and confidentiality, pricing, notice and exit terms.

  6. Scale what works

    Add people or processes once quality is proven, and keep a named lead on both sides.

Questions to ask a Sri Lankan provider

  • Which accounting software and which country's rules has the team worked in?
  • Are staff qualified or studying for CA, CIMA or ACCA, and who reviews their work?
  • How do they access your systems, and what security certifications do they hold?
  • How are month-end deadlines, quality checks and errors handled?
  • Can you meet and choose the people who will work on your account?
  • What does the price include, and what is the notice period?

Common questions

Is Sri Lanka good for accounting outsourcing?

Yes. It has a large base of professionally qualified accountants, with more than 9,000 Chartered Accountants and 40,000 students, plus CIMA and ACCA qualified staff, and global banks such as HSBC and LSEG run finance operations in Colombo.

What accounting work can be outsourced to Sri Lanka?

Bookkeeping, accounts payable and receivable, reconciliations, month-end close, management accounts, FP&A support, audit support and payroll.

Do Sri Lankan accountants know UK, Australian and US accounting?

Many are CIMA, ACCA or CA qualified, which teach IFRS, and firms serving the UK and Australia train staff on local rules such as VAT, GST and BAS. Ask any provider for staff with your specific software and jurisdiction.

How do I start outsourcing bookkeeping to Sri Lanka?

Start with one well documented process, such as accounts payable or reconciliations, with one or two people through a provider, then expand once quality is proven. Tell us your volumes and software and we will introduce suitable teams.

Which are the accounting outsourcing companies in Sri Lanka?

They range from global banks and exchanges running their own finance operations, such as HSBC and the London Stock Exchange Group, to specialist providers serving accounting firms and companies abroad. Our directory of business process companies in Sri Lanka lists examples with sources.

Can I hire an offshore accountant in Sri Lanka for my Australian or UK firm?

Yes. Providers employ the accountant in Sri Lanka and you direct the work, or you can use an employer of record. Ask for experience with BAS, GST and Xero for Australia, or VAT, Making Tax Digital and Sage or Xero for the UK.

Is my financial data safe with a Sri Lankan accounting team?

It depends on the provider's controls, not the country. Ask for ISO 27001 or SOC 2, role based access, no local data storage and a data processing agreement. Sri Lanka's Personal Data Protection Act applies to processors from 1 January 2027.

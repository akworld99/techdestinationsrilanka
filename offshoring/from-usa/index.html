Offshoring

Outsourcing to Sri Lanka from the USA

US companies use Sri Lanka mainly for work that benefits from an overnight turnaround: medical billing and revenue cycle management, investment research, finance operations and data work. Sri Lanka is 9.5 to 13.5 hours ahead of the US, so claims, reconciliations and research processed during the Sri Lankan day are ready when US offices open. Investment research firms in Colombo serve Wall Street institutions, and revenue cycle firms serve US hospitals and practices.

What the USA companies outsource to Sri Lanka

  • Medical billing and RCM: Medical billing, coding, denials and AR follow-up for US providers.
  • Investment research and KPO: Equity and credit research, financial data, fund operations.
  • Accounting and bookkeeping: Bookkeeping, AP and AR, month-end close, management accounts and FP&A.
  • Data annotation and AI data: Labelling, data conversion, LLM evaluation and local language data.
  • Customer support: Email, chat and voice support, customer operations and back office.

Evidence from the USA

  • Acuity Knowledge Partners' Sri Lankan team serves more than 50 global financial institutions. Acuity
  • A 300 seat medical billing and coding centre opened in Kandy in 2024, serving the US, UK and Australia. Daily Mirror
  • Medical transcription teams in Sri Lanka work around the clock for US hospital groups. EDB directory
  • A Colombo company runs a US revenue cycle team on its own billing software. Daily FT

Working hours

Sri Lanka is 9.5 to 10.5 hours ahead of New York, 12.5 to 13.5 hours ahead of California, with no daylight saving.

Contracts, data and compliance

For healthcare work, sign a HIPAA business associate agreement and check the provider's security controls. For other personal data, apply your state privacy obligations through your contract. Common questions

Do US companies outsource to Sri Lanka?

Yes, especially for medical billing, investment research, finance operations and software. The time difference gives an overnight turnaround.

Can a Sri Lankan team work US hours?

Yes, on night shifts, which is standard in US facing healthcare and support work. For back office work, most US clients prefer the overnight model.

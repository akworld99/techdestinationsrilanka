Medical billing and revenue cycle outsourcing to Sri Lanka

Sri Lanka has a long running healthcare revenue cycle industry serving mainly US hospitals and practices. Medical billing, coding, claims submission, payment posting, denials management and AR follow-up are delivered from Colombo and Kandy, where one provider opened a 300 seat centre in 2024. The overnight time difference with the US means claims worked in Sri Lanka are processed by the time US offices open.

AI: what is being automated, and what is not

Transcription is largely being replaced by speech recognition and AI scribes, and charge entry and eligibility checks are increasingly automated. The work that stays human is denials management, complex AR follow-up, certified coding audits and payer communication. Choose a provider that already uses automation and sells those skills.

Common questions

Is Sri Lanka good for medical billing outsourcing?

Yes, especially for US practices and hospital groups. The industry has served US clients for more than a decade, works overnight relative to the US, and has providers with hundreds of seats.

Is outsourcing medical billing to Sri Lanka HIPAA compliant?

It can be. Compliance depends on the provider's controls and the business associate agreement you sign, not the country. Ask for their security certifications and audit history.

What does medical billing outsourcing to Sri Lanka cost?

Providers price per FTE or as a percentage of collections. Ask two or three for quotes on your specialty and volume. We can introduce providers.

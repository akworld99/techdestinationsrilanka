Outsourcing to Sri Lanka from Australia

Australian businesses are one of Sri Lanka's main outsourcing markets, especially accounting firms, professional services and growing companies hiring offshore staff. Sri Lanka is 4.5 to 5.5 hours behind Sydney and 2.5 hours behind Perth, so the Sri Lankan morning overlaps the Australian afternoon and overnight work is ready for the next morning. Remote staffing providers in Colombo employ staff locally for Australian clients, and Australian firms signed new contracts to build Sri Lankan teams in 2026.

What Australia companies outsource to Sri Lanka

  • Accounting and bookkeeping: Bookkeeping, AP and AR, month-end close, management accounts and FP&A.
  • Virtual assistants: Executive and operations assistants, admin and coordination.
  • Digital marketing: SEO, paid media, social, content and marketing operations.
  • Insurance, HR and payroll: Underwriting support, claims, payroll, HR and recruitment.
  • Engineering, BIM and quantity surveying: BIM modelling, CAD drafting, detailing, estimating and BOQs.

Evidence from Australia

  • Sri Lankan staffing firms provide remote staff, employer of record, payroll and recruitment for Australian companies without a local entity. Outsource Accelerator
  • Two Australian companies signed two year contracts in 2026 to build teams in Sri Lanka. Outsource Accelerator
  • A global remote staffing firm opened its Colombo office in 2022 to serve Australian and other clients. EIN Presswire
  • Australia, the UK, the Nordics and North America were named as the main markets of Sri Lanka's business process industry. Echelon

Contracts, data and compliance

The Australian Privacy Principles still apply to personal information you send offshore, so choose a provider with strong security and contract for it. Staff employed by a Sri Lankan provider are employed under Sri Lankan law, and Australian superannuation does not normally apply to them; confirm your position with an adviser. Common questions

Do Australian companies outsource to Sri Lanka?

Yes. Australia is one of the main markets for Sri Lankan business process firms, particularly for accounting, administration, marketing and payroll teams.

What is the time difference between Australia and Sri Lanka?

Sri Lanka is 4.5 hours behind Sydney and Melbourne in winter and 5.5 hours behind during Australian daylight saving, and 2.5 hours behind Perth.

Sri Lanka or the Philippines for Australian offshore staff?

The Philippines is larger and closer in time. Sri Lanka suits Australian firms that want qualified accountants and finance staff, and teams with strong business backgrounds. See our comparison page.

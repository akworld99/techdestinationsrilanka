Legal process outsourcing (LPO) to Sri Lanka

Sri Lankan legal process outsourcing firms provide paralegal support, contract drafting and review, legal research, matter administration and company secretarial work for law firms and in-house teams in the UK, Australia, Singapore and the US. Sri Lankan lawyers train in English in a system with common law roots, which makes them a natural fit for UK and Australian work. This is a small, specialist market: choose providers on the jurisdictions they already serve.

AI: what is being automated, and what is not

First pass document review, clause extraction and research summaries are being automated by legal AI tools. The useful offshore role is now the qualified reviewer: lawyers who check AI output, draft under instruction and run matter administration. Buy legal judgement, not page turning.

What is legal process outsourcing?

Legal process outsourcing (LPO) means using an external team of lawyers and paralegals for legal support work, such as research, document and contract review, drafting under instruction, conveyancing and matter administration, supervised by your own qualified lawyers.

How to outsource legal process outsourcing to Sri Lanka, step by step

  1. Pick one process

    Choose a single, well documented process with clear volumes and quality measures, such as accounts payable or tier two support.

  2. Decide the model

    A managed provider runs it for you; a dedicated team works only for you through a staffing provider or employer of record; your own centre comes later.

  3. Shortlist two or three providers

    Ask each the questions below, check references in your country and meet the people who would do the work.

  4. Run a paid pilot

    Four to eight weeks with clear targets, with your team reviewing output daily at first.

  5. Agree the contract

    Service levels, data protection, IP and confidentiality, pricing, notice and exit terms.

  6. Scale what works

    Add people or processes once quality is proven, and keep a named lead on both sides.

Questions to ask a Sri Lankan provider

Common questions

What is legal process outsourcing?

Sending legal support work, such as research, document and contract review, drafting under instruction and administration, to a specialist team, often offshore, supervised by your own lawyers.

Is Sri Lanka good for legal process outsourcing?

For UK, Australian and Singapore work it can be: lawyers are trained in English in a system with common law roots, and specialist LPO firms already serve those markets. The market is small, so check each provider's experience in your jurisdiction.

Can Sri Lankan paralegals work on UK matters?

Yes, under the supervision of your qualified lawyers, which is how legal process outsourcing works everywhere. Regulated activities stay with your firm.

Is LPO allowed for UK law firms?

Yes. Regulated activities stay with your firm and its qualified lawyers, who supervise and take responsibility for the outsourced work. Check your regulator's guidance on outsourcing and confidentiality.

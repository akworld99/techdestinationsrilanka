Hire virtual assistants and remote admin staff in Sri Lanka

Australian, UK and Gulf businesses hire virtual and executive assistants in Sri Lanka through remote staffing firms that employ the assistant locally and manage payroll, equipment and compliance. The strongest demand comes from Australia, where the time zone gives a shared afternoon, and from the UAE, which works almost the same day. The role is changing fast: routine scheduling and inbox work is being handled by AI, so the best Sri Lankan assistants are hired as operations coordinators who run those tools and own workflows.

AI: what is being automated, and what is not

This is the most exposed category. AI now drafts emails, books meetings, summarises calls and fills spreadsheets. Hiring a low cost assistant to do those tasks by hand no longer makes sense. Hire someone to own outcomes instead: run your AI tools, keep the CRM clean, chase people and coordinate projects. That is the role Sri Lankan assistants with strong English and business backgrounds fill well.

What is hiring a virtual assistant?

A virtual assistant is a remote employee or contractor who handles administrative and operational work, such as calendars, email, research, CRM updates and coordination, for a business or an executive. An offshore virtual assistant does this from another country, usually full time and dedicated to one client.

How to outsource virtual assistants to Sri Lanka, step by step

  1. Pick one process

    Choose a single, well documented process with clear volumes and quality measures, such as accounts payable or tier two support.

  2. Decide the model

    A managed provider runs it for you; a dedicated team works only for you through a staffing provider or employer of record; your own centre comes later.

  3. Shortlist two or three providers

    Ask each the questions below, check references in your country and meet the people who would do the work.

  4. Run a paid pilot

    Four to eight weeks with clear targets, with your team reviewing output daily at first.

  5. Agree the contract

    Service levels, data protection, IP and confidentiality, pricing, notice and exit terms.

  6. Scale what works

    Add people or processes once quality is proven, and keep a named lead on both sides.

Questions to ask a Sri Lankan provider

  • Is the assistant employed by the provider, with leave, equipment and backup covered?
  • Can you interview and choose the person?
  • What tools and AI assistants are they already using?
  • What happens when they are sick or leave?
  • Are there minimum terms or setup fees?

Common questions

How do I hire a virtual assistant from Sri Lanka?

Through a remote staffing provider that employs the assistant locally, through an employer of record, or by contracting a freelancer directly. A provider is easiest for a full time, dedicated assistant. See our guide to paying contractors and employees in Sri Lanka.

Is Sri Lanka or the Philippines better for virtual assistants?

The Philippines is much larger and scores higher on general English rankings. Sri Lanka suits businesses in the UAE, UK and Australia that want assistants with stronger business, finance or operations backgrounds. Our comparison page covers the differences.

Can a Sri Lankan virtual assistant work Australian hours?

Mostly. Sydney is 4.5 to 5.5 hours ahead, so a Sri Lankan assistant starting early overlaps your afternoon fully and your morning partly.

How much does a virtual assistant from Sri Lanka cost?

It depends on experience and whether you hire through a provider or directly. Ask two or three remote staffing providers for a monthly all-in price for the profile you need, and see our salary guide for local pay levels.

Can I find a virtual assistant in Sri Lanka for my business in Dubai?

Yes. Sri Lanka is 1.5 hours ahead of the UAE, so an assistant works almost the same day, and several Sri Lankan providers already serve UAE clients.

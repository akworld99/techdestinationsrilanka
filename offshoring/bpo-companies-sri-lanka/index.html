BPO and outsourcing companies in Sri Lanka (2026)

Sri Lanka's business process industry ranges from the captive centres of global firms, such as HSBC and the London Stock Exchange Group, to specialist providers in accounting, investment research, medical billing, legal support, remote staffing, engineering design and data. The industry body SLASSCOM counts more than 680 IT and BPM companies employing about 175,000 people. Below is who delivers business services from Sri Lanka to overseas clients, grouped by service, each with a public source. We name global firms running their own centres; for specialist providers we describe what exists and introduce buyers directly.

Common questions

What are the biggest BPO companies in Sri Lanka?

Among the largest are the captive centres of global firms such as HSBC and the London Stock Exchange Group, the investment research firm Acuity Knowledge Partners, and global business process firms that have operated in Colombo since 2004, alongside specialist providers in accounting, medical billing, legal support and remote staffing. We introduce buyers to the right ones for their needs.

How many BPO companies are there in Sri Lanka?

The industry body SLASSCOM counts more than 680 IT and BPM companies employing about 175,000 people. There is no current official count of business process firms alone.

Which outsourcing companies in Sri Lanka serve Australian clients?

Several remote staffing, accounting and payroll providers in Colombo serve Australia as a main market, and Australian firms signed new contracts to build Sri Lankan teams in 2026. Tell us what you need and we will introduce two or three that fit.

Are there medical billing companies in Sri Lanka?

Yes. Revenue cycle firms in Colombo and Kandy serve mainly US healthcare providers, including one with a 300 seat Kandy centre opened in 2024.

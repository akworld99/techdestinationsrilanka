BPO and outsourcing companies in Sri Lanka (2026)

Sri Lanka's business process industry ranges from the captive centres of global firms, such as HSBC, the London Stock Exchange Group and EY, to specialist providers in accounting, investment research, medical billing, legal support, remote staffing, engineering design and data. The industry body SLASSCOM counts more than 680 IT and BPM companies employing about 175,000 people. Below are 28 organisations that deliver business services from Sri Lanka to overseas clients, grouped by service, each with a public source.

Common questions

What are the biggest BPO companies in Sri Lanka?

Among the largest business process operations are the captive centres of HSBC, the London Stock Exchange Group and EY, alongside WNS, Acuity Knowledge Partners and specialist firms in accounting, medical billing and legal support. This page lists examples by service, each with a source.

How many BPO companies are there in Sri Lanka?

The industry body SLASSCOM counts more than 680 IT and BPM companies employing about 175,000 people. There is no current official count of business process firms alone.

Which outsourcing companies in Sri Lanka serve Australian clients?

Remote staffing and accounting providers such as Emapta, Formix, H Connect and Sterling BPO list Australia among their markets. See the outsourcing from Australia page for more.

Are there medical billing companies in Sri Lanka?

Yes. Revenue cycle firms in Colombo and Kandy serve mainly US healthcare providers, including one with a 300 seat Kandy centre opened in 2024.

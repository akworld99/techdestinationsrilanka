Investment research and KPO outsourcing to Sri Lanka

Colombo has supported Wall Street and City of London firms for more than two decades. Amba Research, now part of Acuity Knowledge Partners, started in Sri Lanka in 2003, and Acuity's Colombo team serves more than 50 global financial institutions with equity and fixed income research, credit analytics, quantitative work and investment banking support. The London Stock Exchange Group employs more than 1,600 people in Colombo, including data and analytics roles.

AI: what is being automated, and what is not

Data extraction from filings, model roll-forwards and first drafts of earnings notes are increasingly automated. The value of an offshore research team now sits in analysis: credit judgement, alternative data, quantitative work and research tailored to your strategy, with analysts using AI tools rather than replaced by them.

Common questions

Which investment research firms operate in Sri Lanka?

Acuity Knowledge Partners, which began as Amba Research in Colombo in 2003, is the best known. The London Stock Exchange Group and HSBC also run data, analytics and fund operations in Colombo.

Is Sri Lanka a good location for KPO?

Yes for finance led knowledge work. It combines professionally qualified accountants and analysts with more than 20 years of serving global banks, though the pool is smaller than the largest KPO destinations.

Can a small fund or family office use a Sri Lankan research team?

Yes. Dedicated analysts can be hired through a provider or an employer of record without opening an office. Tell us the coverage you need and we will introduce options.

AI: what is being automated, and what is not

Claims intake, data entry for policy administration and payroll calculation are highly automatable. The human value is underwriting support, complex claims, compliance, multi-country payroll rules and candidate experience in recruitment.

What is insurance and HR back office outsourcing?

Insurance and HR back office outsourcing means using an external team for administrative and operational work such as policy and claims administration, underwriting support, payroll processing, HR administration and recruitment support.

How to outsource insurance, hr and payroll to Sri Lanka, step by step

  1. Pick one process

    Choose a single, well documented process with clear volumes and quality measures, such as accounts payable or tier two support.

  2. Decide the model

    A managed provider runs it for you; a dedicated team works only for you through a staffing provider or employer of record; your own centre comes later.

  3. Shortlist two or three providers

    Ask each the questions below, check references in your country and meet the people who would do the work.

  4. Run a paid pilot

    Four to eight weeks with clear targets, with your team reviewing output daily at first.

  5. Agree the contract

    Service levels, data protection, IP and confidentiality, pricing, notice and exit terms.

  6. Scale what works

    Add people or processes once quality is proven, and keep a named lead on both sides.

Questions to ask a Sri Lankan provider

  • Which policy, claims or payroll systems does the team know?
  • Which activities stay with your licensed staff?
  • How are data security and audits handled?
  • What are the service levels and escalation routes?

Can Sri Lankan teams run Australian payroll?

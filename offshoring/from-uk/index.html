Outsourcing to Sri Lanka from the UK

UK businesses have outsourced to Sri Lanka for more than 20 years. HSBC opened its Colombo operations in 2004, the London Stock Exchange Group employs more than 1,600 people there, and in 2025 the UK specialist insurer CFC opened a 200 seat hub in Colombo. Sri Lanka is 4.5 hours ahead of the UK in summer and 5.5 in winter, so a team starting early afternoon covers the full UK day, and work processed in the Sri Lankan morning is ready before London opens.

What the UK companies outsource to Sri Lanka

  • Accounting and bookkeeping: Bookkeeping, AP and AR, month-end close, management accounts and FP&A.
  • Insurance, HR and payroll: Underwriting support, claims, payroll, HR and recruitment.
  • Legal process outsourcing: Paralegal, contract review, legal research and company secretarial.
  • Engineering, BIM and quantity surveying: BIM modelling, CAD drafting, detailing, estimating and BOQs.
  • Customer support: Email, chat and voice support, customer operations and back office.

Contracts, data and compliance

Your UK GDPR obligations still apply when data is processed in Sri Lanka. Use a data processing agreement with international transfer terms, and expect Sri Lanka's own data protection law to apply to processors from 1 January 2027. If you engage individual contractors, take advice on how IR35 applies to your arrangement. For hiring routes and payroll, see how to hire and how to pay people in Sri Lanka.

Common questions

Do UK companies outsource to Sri Lanka?

Yes. HSBC, the London Stock Exchange Group and the specialist insurer CFC all run operations in Colombo, and UK accounting, law and architecture firms use Sri Lankan teams.

What time difference is there between the UK and Sri Lanka?

Sri Lanka is 4.5 hours ahead of the UK in British Summer Time and 5.5 hours ahead in winter. Sri Lanka has no daylight saving.

Is outsourcing to Sri Lanka GDPR compliant?

It can be. Compliance depends on your contract and the provider's controls: a data processing agreement, approved transfer terms and appropriate security. Sri Lanka's Personal Data Protection Act also applies from 1 January 2027.

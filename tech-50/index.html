Sri Lanka Tech 50

The Sri Lanka Tech 50 is our annual list of the fifty companies doing the most to put Sri Lankan technology talent on the world map. Nominations for the first edition are open.

What gets a company on the list

Work delivered for customers outside Sri Lanka, growth in people and revenue, and recognition that can be checked. Every entry is fact checked to the same standard as our stories.

What does not

Payment. Sponsors support the list and are named as sponsors. They have no say in who is selected or how companies are ranked.

How it works

  1. Nominate

    Anyone can nominate a company using the form below.

  2. We verify

    We check the claims against public sources and ask companies for evidence.

  3. We publish

    The fifty are published here and on LinkedIn, each with a short profile and sources.

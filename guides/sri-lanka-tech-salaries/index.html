Sri Lanka tech salary and cost guide 2026

A software engineer in Colombo earns a median of about LKR 177,000 a month (about USD 535) and a senior software engineer about LKR 260,000 (about USD 785), according to self reported Glassdoor data for 2026. Engineers at export focused and dollar pegged employers earn well above that: community reported medians are LKR 280,000 and LKR 447,000. Employers add 15% in statutory contributions.

Last reviewed 10 October 2026. US dollar figures use LKR 331 per dollar, the market rate on 8 October 2026.

Read this first. There is no free, public employer survey of Sri Lankan tech pay. The one that exists, the SLASSCOM Compensation and Benefits Report run by Deloitte, is paid. Everything below is self reported by employees, and the sources disagree with each other. Use the figures as ranges, and expect to pay toward the top for strong people.

What an employee costs on top of salary

  • Employees' Provident Fund: employer pays 12% of gross earnings, employee pays 8% (Department of Labour).
  • Employees' Trust Fund: employer pays 3% (ETF Board, rate per De Saram).
  • Gratuity: half a month's last salary for each completed year, payable when someone with five or more years leaves an employer of 15 or more people (Payment of Gratuity Act).
  • Leave: 14 days annual leave from the third calendar year and 7 days casual leave, plus public holidays. The 2026 government calendar lists 26 public holidays, 8 of them mercantile holidays.
  • Hours: a 45 hour week under the Shop and Office Employees Act.

Worked example: a senior software engineer at the Glassdoor median of LKR 260,000 a month costs about LKR 299,000 with EPF and ETF, or roughly USD 10,800 a year. At the community median of LKR 447,000 the same sum is roughly USD 18,600 a year. Benefits such as medical cover, equipment and office space come on top.

Rates through a partner company

If you take a dedicated team from a Sri Lankan company you pay a rate that includes salary, management, space and margin. The Export Development Board puts developer rates at about USD 25 to 40 an hour and says operations typically cost 40% to 65% less than equivalent headcount in the US or UK. The board promotes exports and cites no study, so treat this as an indication and ask partners for quotes.

Pay rises

The SLASSCOM chairman said in a 2023 interview that normal annual wage growth is about 10% to 15%, and that most tech companies raised pay by 45% to 70% or pegged it to the dollar during the 2022 crisis. SLASSCOM's 2025 report says increment budgets have since stabilised. Budget for double digit annual rises for good engineers.

Common questions

What is the average software engineer salary in Sri Lanka?

Self reported Glassdoor data for Colombo in 2026 shows a median of about LKR 177,000 a month for a software engineer (about USD 535) and LKR 260,000 for a senior software engineer (about USD 785). Community reported figures that include dollar pegged roles are higher, at about LKR 280,000 and LKR 447,000.

What does an employer pay on top of salary in Sri Lanka?

Employers contribute 12% of gross earnings to the Employees' Provident Fund and 3% to the Employees' Trust Fund, 15% in total. Gratuity of half a month's salary per year of service applies once an employee completes five years with an employer of 15 or more people.

How much cheaper is a Sri Lankan engineering team?

The Sri Lanka Export Development Board says operations here typically cost 40% to 65% less than equivalent headcount in the US or UK, with developer rates through vendors of about USD 25 to 40 an hour. It does not cite an underlying study, so treat it as an indication.

Is there an official salary survey for Sri Lankan tech?

The SLASSCOM Compensation and Benefits Report, run by Deloitte, is the main employer survey. It is sold to members and participants and its figures are not public.

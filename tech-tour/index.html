Sri Lanka Tech Tour

The Sri Lanka Tech Tour is a three day visit to Colombo for founders, CTOs and investors who are deciding whether to build a team here. You meet the companies in our stories, see where they work and talk to the people who set them up. We are taking registrations of interest for the first group.

What the three days cover

  1. See the work

    Visits to engineering centres of global companies and Sri Lankan product companies, including a tech park.

  2. Meet the people

    Sessions with founders, engineering leaders and university faculty. Time with engineers, without a sales pitch.

  3. Plan your route

    Briefings on hiring models, costs, company setup and Port City, and one to one time with advisers.

Who it is for

Founders, CTOs, heads of engineering and investors from outside Sri Lanka who are weighing a team of five or more.

How it runs

Small groups, so every visit is a conversation. Dates and cost are set once a group is confirmed, and you decide then whether to join.

Want to prepare first? Read why companies choose Sri Lanka, the salary guide and the setup guide.

Register your interest

No commitment. We will send dates and details when the first group is confirmed.

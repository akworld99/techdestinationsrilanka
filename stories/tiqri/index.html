A Norwegian software company has run its core engineering from Colombo for 15 years. Meet Tiqri

150 developers, 250+ projects and client relationships that have lasted more than a decade.

Tiqri is a Norwegian parented software company that runs its core engineering out of Colombo. For 15+ years, its Sri Lankan developers have built software for clients across Scandinavia, the US, Europe, the Middle East and Asia Pacific.

Dedicated teams, long relationships

The model is deliberate. Tiqri assigns a dedicated, long term engineering team to each client instead of a rotating pool handling tickets. That is why several client relationships have run for over a decade.

The work is real product engineering. For more than ten years, Tiqri's Colombo team has worked alongside a Norway based development team to power end to end automated warehousing: the Automated Storage and Retrieval Systems that cut manual handling, optimise space and improve accuracy across warehouse operations.

The track record

150 developers. 250+ completed projects. A client range spanning early stage startups to Fortune 500 companies. Skillsets cover AI, cloud, enterprise software, IoT, mobile, BI and QA. Leadership blends Norwegian and Sri Lankan expertise, led by CEO Kim Worm-Petersen.

For any founder or CTO watching: a European company chose Sri Lanka as the engine room for 15 years of global software delivery, and trusts it with Fortune 500 work.

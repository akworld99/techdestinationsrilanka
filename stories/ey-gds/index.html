EY runs its global delivery network from nine countries. Sri Lanka is one of them

EY Global Delivery Services employs 90,000+ people worldwide. Its Sri Lanka centre has grown to 500+ staff.

27 May 20261 min readProfessional services

EY's Global Delivery Services network has 90,000+ professionals across 9 countries and 21 cities worldwide.

The countries: India, the Philippines, Poland, Hungary, Argentina, the UK, Mexico, Spain and Sri Lanka.

The Sri Lanka centre

In 2022, EY signed a Board of Investment agreement and opened its GDS centre in Sri Lanka. The workforce is now at 500+ staff.

The centre delivers business consulting, technology consulting and audit services to EY member firms globally.

Sri Lankan professionals at EY GDS work directly with EY teams across every service line, geography and sector, delivering the same quality as their counterparts in London, New York or Mumbai.

Why it matters

When one of the Big Four chooses your country as one of just nine locations globally for its delivery network, that is a strategic bet.

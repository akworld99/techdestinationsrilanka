Why IFS, HCLTech, EY and Sysco keep building in Sri Lanka

These are scaled operations, some of them decades old. Four reasons come up every time.

These are scaled operations that have placed their trust in Sri Lanka for years.

Who is here

IFS has 2,200+ employees in Colombo. They do about 60% of its global product development, by the company's own account, making it the second largest tech employer in the country.

HCLTech opened a delivery center in 2020, then expanded to a facility for 4,000+ employees.

EY runs a Global Delivery Services centre with 500+ staff.

Sysco LABS Sri Lanka builds enterprise software for a Fortune 100 company.

HSBC, WNS, RR Donnelley and Innodata also run operations here.

Why they keep choosing Sri Lanka

The talent pipeline is real. The country produces 12,000+ ICT graduates every year, according to Startup Genome.

English is widely used in business, business culture is familiar to Western companies, and onboarding friction is low from day one.

The time zone works. GMT+5:30 gives four to six hours of daily real time overlap with Europe and three to four hours with the US East Coast. That is enough for standups, code reviews and sprint planning without anyone waking up at 4am.

The cost math is simple. A fully loaded senior engineering team in Colombo runs 60 to 70% cheaper than London, Sydney or San Francisco.

Retention is strong. Engineers join these companies and build entire careers there, because the work is global grade.

When Fortune 500 companies trust your engineers with their core tech, that is the strongest endorsement a market can get.

Sri Lanka's IT and BPM exports hit $1.645 billion in 2025

500+ tech companies and 120,000+ IT professionals make ICT one of the country's largest export earners.

Sri Lanka's IT and BPM sector hit $1.645 billion in export revenue in 2025. January 2026 data showed ICT exports up 60% year on year.

500+ tech companies. 120,000+ IT professionals. Global firms like HSBC, Sysco LABS and HCLTech already run development centers here.

ICT is now one of the country's largest export earners.

This is a $1.6 billion industry that most of the world has not noticed yet. Tech Destination Sri Lanka exists to show why it should.

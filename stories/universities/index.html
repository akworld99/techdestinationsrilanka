The universities behind Sri Lanka's engineering pipeline

Moratuwa has been a top contributor to Google Summer of Code since 2004. SLIIT serves 25,000+ students.

4 May 20261 min readTalent pipeline

2004
Moratuwa in Google Summer of Code since
25,000+
students at SLIIT

The University of Moratuwa is one of the top global contributors to Google Summer of Code, and has been since 2004.

SLIIT, the country's largest private tech university, serves 25,000+ students and was ranked joint third in Sri Lanka by the Times Higher Education World University Rankings.

These are engineers with deep computer science fundamentals, well beyond what a boot camp produces.

Companies like IFS, WSO2 and 99x hire directly from these pipelines, and have done so successfully for decades.

The talent is there. The pipeline is real. The question is whether you are tapping into it.

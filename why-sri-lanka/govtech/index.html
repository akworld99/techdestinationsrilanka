Why Sri Lanka for govtech and public sector technology

Frontwalker, a listed Swedish company that builds public sector software, calls Colombo its global tech center. Surge Global won a technology transformation contract with the government of Qatar. Govtech companies come here for teams that have passed public sector security and procurement scrutiny.

Common questions

Do governments use software built in Sri Lanka?

Yes. Published examples include platforms for the Estonian government by Rootcode, a Qatar government transformation contract won by Surge Global, and Norway's public health record platform, which Creative Software helps build.

Are Sri Lankan suppliers certified for public sector work?

Several are. Rootcode holds ISO 9001 and ISO 27001, and SimCentric holds ISO 27001.

How do public sector suppliers usually engage Sri Lankan teams?

Usually through a dedicated team inside an established company that already holds the certifications the contract requires.

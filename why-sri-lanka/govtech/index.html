Why Sri Lanka

Why Sri Lanka for govtech and public sector technology

Governments already rely on Sri Lankan engineering. A Colombo company works on national AI infrastructure for Estonia, one of the world's most digital governments. A Stockholm listed public sector software group calls Colombo its global tech center. A Sri Lankan firm won a technology transformation contract with the government of Qatar. Govtech companies come here for teams that have passed public sector security and procurement scrutiny.

Common questions

Do governments use software built in Sri Lanka?

Yes. Published examples include platforms for the Estonian government, a Qatar government transformation contract, and Norway's public health record platform.

Are Sri Lankan suppliers certified for public sector work?

Several are. Companies doing public sector work from Colombo hold ISO 9001 and ISO 27001.

How do public sector suppliers usually engage Sri Lankan teams?

Usually through a dedicated team inside an established company that already holds the certifications the contract requires.

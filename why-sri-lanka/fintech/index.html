Why Sri Lanka for fintech and capital markets companies

Some of the most demanding financial software in the world was built by Sri Lankan engineers. The London Stock Exchange chose MillenniumIT for its trading platform and then bought the company. JPMorganChase acquired WealthOS, a wealth infrastructure platform founded by two Sri Lankans, in 2026. Virtusa serves four of the top five banks in the US from a 3,000+ engineer base in Colombo. Fintech companies come here for engineers who understand low latency, regulation and scale.

Common questions

Which financial platforms were built in Sri Lanka?

The Millennium Exchange trading platform used by the London Stock Exchange was built by MillenniumIT in Colombo. WealthOS, acquired by JPMorganChase in 2026, built its wealth infrastructure with a 50+ engineer team in Sri Lanka.

Do global banks use Sri Lankan engineers?

Yes. Virtusa serves four of the top five US banks with a large engineering base in Colombo, and JPMorganChase took on the WealthOS team in Sri Lanka.

Can I hire finance operations staff as well as engineers?

Yes. Infomate and WNS run finance and accounting services for international clients from Sri Lanka, and the country has deep CIMA and ACCA talent.

Why Sri Lanka

Why Sri Lanka for fintech and capital markets companies

Some of the most demanding financial software in the world was built by Sri Lankan engineers. The London Stock Exchange chose a trading platform built in Colombo and then bought the company behind it. JPMorganChase acquired a wealth infrastructure platform founded by two Sri Lankans in 2026. Engineers in Colombo serve four of the top five US banks. Fintech companies come here for engineers who understand low latency, regulation and scale.

Common questions

Which financial platforms were built in Sri Lanka?

The Millennium Exchange trading platform used by the London Stock Exchange was built by MillenniumIT in Colombo. WealthOS, acquired by JPMorganChase in 2026, built its wealth infrastructure with a 50+ engineer team in Sri Lanka.

Do global banks use Sri Lankan engineers?

Yes. Virtusa serves four of the top five US banks with a large engineering base in Colombo, and JPMorganChase took on the WealthOS team in Sri Lanka.

Can I hire finance operations staff as well as engineers?

Yes. WNS and Sri Lankan outsourcing firms run finance and accounting services for international clients from Sri Lanka, and the country has deep CIMA and ACCA talent.

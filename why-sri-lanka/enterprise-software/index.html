Why Sri Lanka

Why Sri Lanka for enterprise software and ERP companies

Enterprise software is the deepest part of Sri Lanka's tech industry. IFS does about 60% of its global product development in Colombo. WSO2, an API and identity software company founded and headquartered here, sold for $600 million in 2024. Sysco builds the core systems of the world's largest foodservice distributor here, on the same codebase as its US teams. Software companies come here for product engineers who have maintained large systems for years.

What is already built in Sri Lanka

  • About 60% of IFS global product development, done by 2,200+ people in Colombo.
  • API management, integration and identity software used by 700+ enterprises in 70+ countries, built and led from Colombo at WSO2.
  • Sysco's core systems, on the same codebase, sprint cycles and engineering standards as its teams in Texas.
  • Dedicated product teams for Scandinavian software companies, some of them running for more than 20 years.

An ERP talent pool built over 27 years

IFS has operated in Sri Lanka for 27 years. Its alumni have founded and staffed consultancies and product companies across Colombo, which is why ERP skills are easier to find here than in most markets of this size.

Investors came back for more

EQT, Hg and TA Associates, the owners of IFS, launched a second company in Colombo in 2025 to serve the rest of their software portfolio from Sri Lanka. EQT also owns WSO2.

Common questions

Which enterprise software companies build in Sri Lanka?

IFS, WSO2, Sysco and Virtusa all run large engineering operations in Colombo.

Can I find IFS and ERP consultants in Sri Lanka?

Yes. IFS has built its largest R&D base here over 27 years, and a number of Sri Lankan firms employ engineers and consultants who came through that ecosystem.

Is Sri Lanka only used for support work?

No. IFS does about 60% of its global product development in Colombo, WSO2 builds its whole product here, and Sysco LABS shares one codebase with its US teams.

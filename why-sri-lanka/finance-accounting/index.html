Why Sri Lanka

Why Sri Lanka for finance, accounting and shared services

Sri Lanka has one of the largest pools of globally certified accountants outside the UK, and global firms have built on it for two decades. WNS, now part of Capgemini, has delivered from Colombo since 2004. EY chose Sri Lanka as one of nine countries for its global delivery network. Sri Lankan firms run finance operations for clients in 10+ countries. Companies come here for qualified finance professionals who work on SAP, Oracle, NetSuite and Microsoft Dynamics every day.

Automation changes the work, and the teams have adapted

Routine finance work is being automated everywhere. The firms here have built that automation themselves. In healthcare revenue operations, for example, machine learning handles the volume, while certified professionals handle the judgment, the exceptions and the accountability.

Beyond Colombo

Finance and BPO centres operate in Jaffna, Kandy and rural towns as well as Colombo. Talent is available outside the capital.

Common questions

Why is Sri Lanka strong in accounting talent?

It has one of the largest pools of CIMA, ACCA and CA qualified professionals outside the UK, and international finance operations have run here since 2004.

Which global firms run shared services in Sri Lanka?

EY Global Delivery Services, WNS (part of Capgemini), Michelin and HSBC all run delivery or shared service operations in Sri Lanka.

Will AI replace outsourced finance work?

Automation is taking the routine volume. Providers in Sri Lanka are building that automation themselves and moving their people to exceptions, judgment and compliance work.

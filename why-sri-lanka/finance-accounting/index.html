Why Sri Lanka

Why Sri Lanka for finance, accounting and shared services

Sri Lanka has one of the largest pools of globally certified accountants outside the UK, and global firms have built on it for two decades. Infomate runs finance operations for 150+ clients in 10+ countries. WNS has delivered from Colombo since 2004. EY chose Sri Lanka as one of nine countries for its global delivery network. Companies come here for qualified finance professionals who work on SAP, Oracle, NetSuite and Microsoft Dynamics every day.

Common questions

Why is Sri Lanka strong in accounting talent?

It has one of the largest pools of CIMA, ACCA and CA qualified professionals outside the UK, and firms such as Infomate have run international finance operations here since 2005.

Which global firms run shared services in Sri Lanka?

EY Global Delivery Services, WNS (part of Capgemini), Michelin, HSBC and Infomate all run delivery or shared service operations in Sri Lanka.

Will AI replace outsourced finance work?

Automation is taking the routine volume. Providers in Sri Lanka are building that automation themselves and moving their people to exceptions, judgment and compliance work.

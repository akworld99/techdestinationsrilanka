Why Sri Lanka

Why Sri Lanka for automotive tech companies

Sri Lankan engineers already contribute to European vehicle software: embedded systems, electric vehicle software and software defined vehicle platforms, for a supplier whose work reaches brands including Volkswagen, BMW, Audi, Mercedes and DAF. A Sri Lankan team also designed and built an all electric supercar and the country's largest EV charging network. Automotive companies come here for embedded and systems engineers who can work to safety standards.

What is already built in Sri Lanka

  • Embedded systems, electric vehicle software and software defined vehicle platforms for European vehicle programmes.
  • An all electric supercar and a national EV charging network, designed and built locally.
  • Emulation and prototyping tools that chip designers use to verify automotive silicon, developed at Synopsys in Colombo.
  • IT services for Michelin's operations across Asia.

From enterprise software to vehicle software

One Colombo engineering company was founded in 2019 by enterprise software engineers. Within a few years its team was contributing to safety critical automotive work. That shift shows how quickly strong general engineers here move into embedded and vehicle domains when a partner brings the programme.

Hardware is part of the picture

A locally designed electric supercar and a national charging network show that vehicle, power electronics and charging work has been done end to end in Sri Lanka, beyond pure software.

Common questions

Do Sri Lankan engineers work on automotive software today?

Yes. Engineers in Colombo contribute to embedded, electric vehicle and software defined vehicle projects through a partnership with ICT Automotive, a European supplier to major vehicle brands.

Is there electric vehicle experience in Sri Lanka?

Yes. An all electric supercar and the country's largest EV charging network were designed and built in Sri Lanka.

What is the best way for an automotive supplier to start?

Start with a small embedded team inside a partner company that already works to automotive processes, and grow it as the programme scales.

Why Sri Lanka

Why Sri Lanka for automotive tech companies

Sri Lankan engineers are already contributing to European vehicle software. Inivos works with ICT Automotive, a supplier behind software for brands including Volkswagen, BMW, Audi, Mercedes and DAF, on embedded systems, electric vehicle software and software defined vehicle platforms. CodeGen designed and built Vega, an all electric supercar, and the country's largest EV charging network. Automotive companies come here for embedded and systems engineers who can work to safety standards.

From enterprise software to vehicle software

Inivos was founded in 2019 by engineers who came up through IFS. Within a few years its team was contributing to safety critical automotive work. That shift shows how quickly strong general engineers here move into embedded and vehicle domains when a partner brings the programme.

Hardware is part of the picture

CodeGen's Vega and ChargeNET show that vehicle, power electronics and charging work has been done end to end in Sri Lanka, beyond pure software.

Common questions

Do Sri Lankan engineers work on automotive software today?

Yes. Inivos engineers contribute to embedded, electric vehicle and software defined vehicle projects through a partnership with ICT Automotive, a European supplier to major vehicle brands.

Is there electric vehicle experience in Sri Lanka?

Yes. CodeGen built the Vega electric supercar and the ChargeNET charging network in Sri Lanka.

What is the best way for an automotive supplier to start?

Start with a small embedded team inside a partner company that already works to automotive processes, and grow it as the programme scales.

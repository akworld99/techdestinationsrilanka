Why Sri Lanka for AI companies

Sri Lankan teams now build AI products as well as services. Colombo companies ship their own AI developer tools and agentic software engineering platforms, and one is helping build national AI infrastructure for the Estonian government. AI companies come here for engineers who already build with frontier models in production.

Services firms that moved first

Engineering firms here restructured how their engineers work alongside AI in 2025, and some then started building their own AI products. Teams here use AI in delivery daily, so you are hiring people who already work the way you do.

Local AI infrastructure exists

AI model hosting is now available on infrastructure in Sri Lanka, billed in rupees. For companies that need regional hosting or local deployment, the base layer is in place.

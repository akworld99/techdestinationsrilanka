Why Sri Lanka

Why Sri Lanka for semiconductor and embedded software companies

Chip design and device software are engineered in Sri Lanka today. Synopsys, the global leader in electronic design automation, has run R&D in Colombo since 2015, working on logic synthesis, FPGA prototyping and emulation. Zebra Technologies builds software for its scanners and mobile computers at its Colombo R&D center. Semiconductor and embedded companies come here for engineers trained in a university curriculum that Synopsys helped design.

Common questions

Is chip design work done in Sri Lanka?

Yes. Synopsys has engineered parts of its electronic design automation software in Colombo since 2015, including synthesis, prototyping and emulation products.

Can I hire embedded engineers in Sri Lanka?

Yes. Zebra Technologies, Inivos and SimCentric all employ embedded, device or simulation engineers in Colombo.

Do Sri Lankan universities teach VLSI?

Yes. Moratuwa, Peradeniya and Ruhuna built a VLSI curriculum together with Synopsys.

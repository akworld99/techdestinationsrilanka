Why Sri Lanka

Why Sri Lanka for semiconductor and embedded software companies

Chip design and device software are engineered in Sri Lanka today. Synopsys, the global leader in electronic design automation, has run R&D in Colombo since 2015, working on logic synthesis, FPGA prototyping and emulation. Zebra Technologies builds software for its scanners and mobile computers at its Colombo R&D centre. Semiconductor and embedded companies come here for engineers trained in a university curriculum that Synopsys helped design.

What is already built in Sri Lanka

  • Core product work at Synopsys on RTL compilation, logic synthesis, HAPS prototyping and the ZeBu emulation platform.
  • A wholly owned Zebra Technologies software R&D centre building for its global device platforms across mobile, cloud and IoT.
  • Embedded systems and vehicle software for European automotive programmes.
  • High fidelity simulation software on Unreal Engine, engineered in Colombo under ISO 27001.

Common questions

Is chip design work done in Sri Lanka?

Yes. Synopsys has engineered parts of its electronic design automation software in Colombo since 2015, including synthesis, prototyping and emulation products.

Can I hire embedded engineers in Sri Lanka?

Yes. Zebra Technologies and several Sri Lankan companies employ embedded, device or simulation engineers in Colombo.

Do Sri Lankan universities teach VLSI?

Yes. Moratuwa, Peradeniya and Ruhuna built a VLSI curriculum together with Synopsys.
